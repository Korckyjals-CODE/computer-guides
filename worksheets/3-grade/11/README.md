# Safe Use – Worksheets (Grade 3, Week 11)

## For the teacher

- **Use:** Whenever the topic of safe use of technology fits your curriculum. Self-contained; not tied to a computer project.
- **Format:** Offline, pen and paper (color pencils optional). About **25 minutes** per worksheet.
- **Guidance:** Students can complete the activities on their own.
- **Standalone value:** Each worksheet teaches on its own.

## Learning objectives

### Technical
- Explain what **safe use** means for a device.
- Practice safe sitting and handling habits.
- Keep **passwords** private and log out on shared computers.
- Pause and ask before clicking odd pop-ups or links.
- Show three safe-use steps in a drawing or list.

### Social
- Take **fair turns** with shared devices.
- Choose **kind** words and ask before sharing photos.
- **Tell a trusted adult** about spills, cracks, or scary messages.
- Name actions that help or hurt the group.
- Create a short class safe-use sign.
