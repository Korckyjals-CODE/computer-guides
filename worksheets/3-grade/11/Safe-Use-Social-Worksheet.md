## Part 2 – Fair turns keep everyone safe (about 5 minutes)

Rushing or grabbing can cause drops and hard feelings. **Fair turns** mean waiting, asking, and leaving the device ready for the next person.

**Real-life scenario:** Two students both want the last tablet. One pulls it from the other’s hands. The tablet falls. Both feel upset, and class time is lost.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** What could each student say to share fairly?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Mark **Helps the group** or **Hurts the group**.

| Action | Helps or Hurts? |
|--------|-----------------|
| Wait in line for the tablet cart | _____ |
| Grab a tablet from someone’s hands | _____ |
| Return the device plugged in when done | _____ |
| Keep a shared tablet past your turn on purpose | _____ |

## Part 3 – Kind words around technology (about 5 minutes)

How we talk about people online and offline matters. Do not post or share photos of classmates without permission. Do not tease someone about a mistake on a screen.

**Real-life scenario:** Ana misspells a word on the class board app. Another student laughs loudly and says her mistake to the whole room. Ana wants to stop trying.

**Critical thinking:** Write one kind thing a classmate could say instead.

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Circle **Kind** or **Not kind**.

| Action | Kind or Not kind? |
|--------|-------------------|
| Ask before taking a classmate’s photo | Kind · Not kind |
| Share a funny video of someone without asking | Kind · Not kind |
| Help a partner fix a typing error quietly | Kind · Not kind |

---

## Part 4 – Speak up for safety (about 5 minutes)

If you see a spill, a cracked screen, a mean message, or a scary pop-up, **tell a trusted adult**. Speaking up protects the whole group. You are not “tattling”—you are helping.

**Real-life scenario:** Sam sees water near a keyboard. Sam did not spill it. Sam worries that telling will make someone angry.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** What could Sam say to the teacher?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Number the helpful steps 1, 2, and 3.

| Step | Order (1, 2, or 3) |
|------|--------------------|
| Ask an adult for help | _____ |
| Stay calm and do not touch the spill or pop-up | _____ |
| Notice something unsafe | _____ |
