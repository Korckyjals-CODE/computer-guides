## How to use this worksheet

- Do the parts **in order** from top to bottom.
- Each part has a **time** so you know how long to spend.
- Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

---

## Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

**What you will learn:** Good names help the whole class. When everyone writes clear names, we find files faster and feel less stress.

## Part 2 – Names that help everyone (about 5 minutes)

If a class folder has clear names, a substitute teacher can find anything. This saves time.

**Real-life scenario:** The teacher is away. The substitute cannot find the homework files.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** What could the class do to help?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Circle **Helps** or **Does not help**.

| Choice | Result |
|---|---|
| Clear names | Helps · Does not help |
| Secret codes | Helps · Does not help |
| Topic in the name | Helps · Does not help |
| Names like “thing” | Helps · Does not help |

---

## Part 3 – Being kind in names (about 5 minutes)

Names on shared files should be **respectful**. No jokes about others or mean words.

**Real-life scenario:** A file called “Sam is silly” is in the class folder.

**Critical thinking:** How might Sam feel?

_________________________________________

**Word bank:** *kind* · *respect* · *joke*

**Do this:** Fill in the best word.

| Sentence | Word |
|---|---|
| Names should be ... | _____ |
