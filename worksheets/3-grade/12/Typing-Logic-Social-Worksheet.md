## Part 2 – Partners and patience (about 5 minutes)

When you share a keyboard, agree who types and who checks. Rushing a partner makes more mistakes and hurt feelings.

**Real-life scenario:** Omar checks spelling while Jade types. Omar says “Take your time—look for the space.” Jade finishes a clear sentence.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** What happens if a partner shouts “Hurry!” during typing?

## Part 3 – Kind feedback on typos (about 5 minutes)

Everyone makes typos. Kind feedback names the fix (“add a space”) instead of calling someone “bad at typing.”

**Real-life scenario:** A classmate types “teh”. One friend says “You’re so dumb.” Another says “Swap e and h—the.”

**Critical thinking:** Which comment helps learning more? Why?

## Part 4 – Shared work must be readable (about 5 minutes)

Group slides and shared docs need clear typing so the whole team can use the work. Messy text wastes everyone’s time.

**Real-life scenario:** A team poster says “Wecolme to our class”. Visitors laugh; the team feels embarrassed.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Whose job is it to check spelling before sharing with the class?
