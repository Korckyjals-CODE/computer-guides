## Part 2 – Spaces and word order (about 5 minutes)

Words need **spaces** between them. Letters inside a word stay together. Typing logic says: finish a word → space → next word.

**Real-life scenario:** Tara types “Ilovemy dog” as one blob, then “I love my dog” with spaces. The second line is easy to read.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Why does a missing space confuse a reader?

## Part 3 – Capitals with Shift (about 5 minutes)

A **capital letter** often starts a sentence or a name. Hold **Shift** and press a letter key at the same time. That is typing logic for capitals—not Caps Lock for every letter.

**Real-life scenario:** Ben wants “Maria”. He holds Shift + M, then types aria without Shift.

**Critical thinking:** What is wrong with leaving Caps Lock on for a whole sentence of names and normal words?

_________________________________________

**Do this:**

1. **Write one name** that needs a capital letter.

   _________________________

2. **Circle** the better habit: Shift for one capital · Caps Lock for every letter always

## Part 4 – Fix mistakes in order (about 5 minutes)

When you see a typo, move to it (or use Backspace from the end) and fix **one mistake at a time**. Random mashing of keys creates more errors.

**Real-life scenario:** Lila typed “freind”. She uses Backspace to erase d-n-i-e-r-f and retypes “friend” carefully.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Why is slow careful fixing better than slamming many keys?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Circle **Yes** or **No**.

| Action | Logical? Circle |
|---|---|
| Read the word, then Backspace the wrong letters | Yes · No |
| Press many random keys hoping it looks right | Yes · No |
| Check spaces after you fix spelling | Yes · No |
