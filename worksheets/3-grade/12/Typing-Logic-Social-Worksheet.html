Typing Logic: Social Worksheet

Grade 3 · Worksheet · About 25 minutes
Work with pen and paper. Use color pencils if you like. You do not need a computer or the internet.

Name: Grade/Section: Date:
How to use this worksheet
  • Do the parts in order from top to bottom.
  • Each part has a time so you know how long to spend.
  • Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: Clear typing helps readers—classmates and teachers. Typing logic is also social: patient partners, fair sharing of the keyboard, and kind comments about mistakes.

Do this:

  1. Name someone who reads what you type at school.
  2. Circle a kind goal: Help each other type clearly · Laugh at every typo · Hide the keyboard

Part 2 – Partners and patience (about 5 minutes)

When you share a keyboard, agree who types and who checks. Rushing a partner makes more mistakes and hurt feelings.

Real-life scenario: Omar checks spelling while Jade types. Omar says “Take your time—look for the space.” Jade finishes a clear sentence.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: What happens if a partner shouts “Hurry!” during typing?

Part 3 – Kind feedback on typos (about 5 minutes)

Everyone makes typos. Kind feedback names the fix (“add a space”) instead of calling someone “bad at typing.”

Real-life scenario: A classmate types “teh”. One friend says “You’re so dumb.” Another says “Swap e and h—the.”

Critical thinking: Which comment helps learning more? Why?

Do this:

  1. Rewrite a kind tip for a friend who forgot a capital at the start.
  2. Circle the kinder line: “Fix the first letter with Shift.” · “Only babies forget capitals.”

Part 4 – Shared work must be readable (about 5 minutes)

Group slides and shared docs need clear typing so the whole team can use the work. Messy text wastes everyone’s time.

Real-life scenario: A team poster says “Wecolme to our class”. Visitors laugh; the team feels embarrassed.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: Whose job is it to check spelling before sharing with the class?

Part 5 – Class tip card (about 5 minutes)

Do this: Draw a tip card for the class about kind typing logic (spaces, Shift, patient partners).

Write your tip in one short sentence.

Part 6 – Check what you learned (about 2 minutes)

Checklist. Put a ✓ in the box when it is true for you.

  • □ I know clear typing helps readers.
  • □ I can be a patient typing partner.
  • □ I give kind feedback on typos.
  • □ I check shared text before the class sees it.
  • □ I can make a short tip card.

When all boxes are checked, you have reviewed the main ideas from this worksheet.
