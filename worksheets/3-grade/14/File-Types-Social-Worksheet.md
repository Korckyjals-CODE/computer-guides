## How to use this worksheet

- Do the parts **in order** from top to bottom.
- Each part has a **time** so you know how long to spend.
- Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

---

## Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

**What you will learn:** File types affect how we share with others. Choosing the right type helps classmates open our work, and respecting file rules keeps everyone safe.

## Part 2 – Sharing in the right type (about 5 minutes)

If a friend's computer cannot open your file type, they cannot see your work.

**Real-life scenario:** Ana sends a drawing in a rare type. Leo cannot open it.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** What could Ana do instead?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Circle **Helpful** or **Not helpful**.

| Choice | Helpful? |
|---|---|
| Use a common type like .jpg | Helpful · Not helpful |

## Part 3 – Safe files (about 5 minutes)

Unknown files can be unsafe. We open only files from people we trust.

**Real-life scenario:** A stranger sends a file named Prize.exe.

**Critical thinking:** What should you do?

_________________________________________
