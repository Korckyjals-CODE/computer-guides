Everyday Automation

Grade 3 · Worksheet · About 25 minutes
Work with pen and paper. Use color pencils if you like. You do not need a computer or the internet.

Name: Grade/Section: Date:
How to use this worksheet
  • Do the parts in order from top to bottom.
  • Each part has a time so you know how long to spend.
  • Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: In this worksheet you will learn what automation means, how machines can do repeated jobs, and why automation can help people save time and effort.

Do this:

  1. Write 2 jobs that a machine can do for people.
  2. In one short sentence, why is it helpful when a machine does a task over and over?

Part 2 – What is automation? (about 5 minutes)

Automation is when a machine or a system does a task automatically, without a person doing every step. A washing machine, a toaster, or a vending machine can do jobs with little help from people.

Do this:

  1. Circle 2 machines that can help with everyday jobs:
    Washing machine     Toaster     Pencil     Clock
  2. Write one more example of an automatic machine you have seen.

Part 3 – Everyday automation at home (about 6 minutes)

Many tools in our homes are automatic. A dishwasher cleans dishes on its own. A microwave heats food. A vacuum cleaner can move around and clean the floor. These machines save time and help people with chores.

Do this:

  1. Match the machine to the job. Write the correct letter in each blank.
    A. Microwave   B. Washing machine   C. Dishwasher
    - Cleans dishes:
    - Washes clothes:
    - Heats food:
  2. In one short sentence, which machine do you think is most helpful at home and why?

Part 4 – Automation can help people (about 5 minutes)

Automation helps people because machines can do the same job many times without getting tired. This can save time, energy, and effort. Sometimes automation makes work safer too.

Do this:

  1. Solve this: If a machine sorts 10 toys in 2 minutes, and a person sorts 10 toys in 8 minutes, which is faster? Circle one:
    The machine     The person
  2. In one short sentence, why is it useful that machines can repeat the same task many times?

Part 5 – Automation in school and the community (about 4 minutes)

Automation is not only at home. It can also be used in schools, stores, and public places. For example, a library may have a machine that sorts books, and a school may use a system to open and close doors or to control lights.

Do this:

  1. Draw a machine that helps people in a school, store, or home. It can be a toaster, a robot, a smart door, or another idea.

In one short sentence, how does your machine help people?

Part 6 – Check what you learned (about 2 minutes)
