## Part 2 – Access and inclusion (about 5 minutes)

If a government service exists only online, people without internet or devices may be excluded. This is the **digital divide**.

**Real-life scenario:** An office moves all procedures online. Elders and rural families struggle.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** What can the organization do?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Circle **Inclusive** or **Excludes**.

| Choice | Effect |
|---|---|
| Offer in-person help | Inclusive · Excludes |
| Online only | Inclusive · Excludes |
| Offer phone support | Inclusive · Excludes |
| Hard-to-read forms | Inclusive · Excludes |

## Part 3 – Privacy and surveillance (about 5 minutes)

Systems can collect much data. Responsible organizations collect **only what they need**, protect it and explain its use.

**Real-life scenario:** An app asks for access to contacts and photos it does not need.

**Critical thinking:** What principle is being ignored?

_________________________________________

**Word bank:** *minimize* · *protect* · *explain*

**Do this:** Fill in the best word.

| Sentence | Word |
|---|---|
| Collect the least data to ... | _____ |
| We must ... the data | _____ |
| We should ... how data is used | _____ |

## Part 4 – Impact on work (about 5 minutes)

Automation changes jobs. Some tasks disappear and new roles appear. Ethical organizations plan **training** and **fair transitions**.

**Real-life scenario:** A company automates invoicing and lets staff go without notice.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** What would a fairer approach look like?
