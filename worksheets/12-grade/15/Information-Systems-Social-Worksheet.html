Information Systems: Social Worksheet

Grade 12 · Worksheet · About 25 minutes
Work with pen and paper. Use color pencils if you like. You do not need a computer or the internet.

Name: Grade/Section: Date:
How to use this worksheet
  • Do the parts in order from top to bottom.
  • Each part has a time so you know how long to spend.
  • Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: Information systems shape society: they affect jobs, privacy, access and power. Designers and users share responsibility for ethical, inclusive and sustainable systems.

Do this:

  1. Name one group that may be left out by a digital system.
  2. Circle a responsible design aim: Inclusion and fairness · Fastest profit only

Part 2 – Access and inclusion (about 5 minutes)

If a government service exists only online, people without internet or devices may be excluded. This is the digital divide.

Real-life scenario: An office moves all procedures online. Elders and rural families struggle.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: What can the organization do?

Do this: Circle Inclusive or Excludes.

ChoiceEffect
Offer in-person helpInclusive · Excludes
Online onlyInclusive · Excludes
Offer phone supportInclusive · Excludes

Part 3 – Privacy and surveillance (about 5 minutes)

Systems can collect much data. Responsible organizations collect only what they need, protect it and explain its use.

Real-life scenario: An app asks for access to contacts and photos it does not need.

Critical thinking: What principle is being ignored?

Word bank: minimize · protect · explain

Do this: Fill in the best word.

SentenceWord
Collect the least data to ...
We must ... the data
We should ... how data is used

Part 4 – Impact on work (about 5 minutes)

Automation changes jobs. Some tasks disappear and new roles appear. Ethical organizations plan training and fair transitions.

Real-life scenario: A company automates invoicing and lets staff go without notice.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: What would a fairer approach look like?

Do this:

  1. Write two actions to support workers when systems change.
  2. Circle the responsible action: Offer retraining · Surprise layoffs

Part 5 – Society map (about 5 minutes)

Do this: Draw a map showing how an information system touches users, workers and community.

Mark one risk and one benefit.

Part 6 – Check what you learned (about 2 minutes)

Checklist. Put a ✓ in the box when it is true for you.

  • □ I understand the digital divide.
  • □ I know privacy principles.
  • □ I think about work impact.
  • □ I support inclusion.
  • □ I can weigh risks and benefits.

When all boxes are checked, you have reviewed the main ideas from this worksheet.
