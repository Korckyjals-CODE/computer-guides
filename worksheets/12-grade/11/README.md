# System Resilience – Worksheets (Grade 12, Week 11)

## For the teacher

- **Use:** Whenever system resilience fits your curriculum. Self-contained; not tied to a computer project.
- **Format:** Offline, pen and paper. About **25 minutes** per worksheet.
- **Guidance:** Students can work independently.
- **Standalone value:** Each worksheet teaches on its own.

## Files

### Technical
- **System-Resilience-Technical-Worksheet.html** / **.md**

### Social
- **System-Resilience-Social-Worksheet.html** / **.md**

## Learning objectives

### Technical
- Distinguish uptime from resilience.
- Explain redundancy, backup, failover, and monitoring.
- Identify SPOFs and discuss RTO/RPO ideas.
- Sketch a resilient mini-architecture.

### Social
- Treat people and process as part of resilience.
- Assign outage roles; plan for unequal impact.
- Argue for drills and transparent communication.
- Outline a community resilience board.
