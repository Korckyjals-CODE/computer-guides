## Part 2 – People as part of the resilient system (about 5 minutes)

Operators, teachers, students, and vendors are part of the system. Resilience includes knowing **who decides**, **who communicates**, and **who can escalate**.

**Real-life scenario:** The learning platform fails during finals week. IT knows a workaround. Teachers are not told for three hours. Rumors spread that grades were deleted.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Which human/process gaps reduced resilience even if servers could be fixed quickly?
