## Part 2 – Core components (about 5 minutes)

**Sources** produce data. **ETL pipelines** move and clean it. **Warehouses** hold structured data for analysis, while **lakes** hold raw data of many formats.

**Real-life scenario:** A store sends sales data through an ETL pipeline into a warehouse.
