Data Architecture: Social Worksheet

Grade 12 · Worksheet · About 25 minutes
Work with pen and paper. Use color pencils if you like. You do not need a computer or the internet.

Name: Grade/Section: Date:
How to use this worksheet
  • Do the parts in order from top to bottom.
  • Each part has a time so you know how long to spend.
  • Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: Architecture decisions shape privacy, fairness, and who benefits. Architects must design with ethics, accessibility, and accountability from the start.

Do this:

  1. Name one ethical concern in a data system.
  2. Circle the design approach that respects users: Privacy by design · Add privacy later

Part 2 – Privacy by design (about 5 minutes)

Build privacy into the architecture from the start: collect less, encrypt, and limit access.

Real-life scenario: A system stores full birth dates when only age is needed.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: What would you change?

Do this: Circle Better or Worse.

Design choiceBetter or worse?
Collect only ageBetter · Worse
Store everything foreverBetter · Worse
Encrypt personal dataBetter · Worse
Open access to all staffBetter · Worse

Part 3 – Access and equity (about 5 minutes)

Dashboards and tools should be usable by people with different needs and skills.

Real-life scenario: A dashboard is unreadable for color-blind users.

Critical thinking: How could the design improve?

Word bank: Accessible · Inclusive · Equity

Do this: Write the best word.

SituationWord
Design for ... use
Include every ... user
Aim for ... of access

Part 4 – Accountability and transparency (about 5 minutes)

Document data flows so anyone can explain where data came from and who used it.

Real-life scenario: A regulator asks how a decision was made. The team shows a data lineage diagram.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: Why is lineage valuable?

Do this:

  1. Write one thing that should be documented.
  2. Circle the stronger practice: Document data flows · Keep them secret

Part 5 – Ethical architecture (about 5 minutes)

Do this: Draw an architecture with three ethical safeguards.

Write which safeguard you would add first.

Part 6 – Check what you learned (about 2 minutes)

Checklist. Put a ✓ in the box when it is true for you.

  • □ I design for privacy.
  • □ I design for access.
  • □ I support transparency.
  • □ I document flows.
  • □ I weigh ethics in architecture.

When all boxes are checked, you have reviewed the main ideas from this worksheet.
