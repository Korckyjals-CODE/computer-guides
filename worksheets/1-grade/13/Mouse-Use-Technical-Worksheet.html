Mouse Use: Technical Worksheet

Grade 1 · Worksheet · About 25 minutes
Work with pen and paper. Use color pencils if you like. You do not need a computer or the internet.

Name: Grade/Section: Date:
How to use this worksheet
  • Do the parts in order from top to bottom.
  • Each part has a time so you know how long to spend.
  • Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: A mouse is a tool you hold in your hand. When you move it, a small arrow called the pointer moves on the screen. The buttons on the mouse help you choose things.

Do this:

  1. Name one place where you have seen a computer mouse.
  2. Circle the tool that moves the pointer: Mouse · Banana

Part 2 – What a mouse has (about 5 minutes)

The mouse has a left button, a right button, and a little wheel. The left button is the one we use most.

Real-life scenario: Lia moves her mouse to the right. The arrow on the screen also goes to the right.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: What happens to the arrow when you move the mouse to the left?

Do this: Circle Yes or No.

Part 3 – Pointer and move (about 5 minutes)

The pointer is the little arrow. We move the mouse slowly and gently to guide the pointer.

Real-life scenario: Tom pushes the mouse very fast. The pointer jumps and he cannot find it.

Critical thinking: Why is it better to move the mouse slowly?

Word bank: Pointer · Button · Mouse

Do this: Fill in the word that matches.

ClueWord
The arrow on the screen
The tool you hold in your hand
The part you press

Part 4 – Click (about 5 minutes)

A click means pressing a button on the mouse one time. Two quick clicks are a double click.

Real-life scenario: Mia clicks on a picture. The picture opens.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: What do you click when you want to choose something?

Do this:

  1. Write the number of clicks for a double click.
  2. Circle the better way to hold a mouse: Gentle hand · Tight fist

Part 5 – My mouse drawing (about 5 minutes)

Do this: Draw a mouse with its two buttons and the pointer on a screen.

Write one rule for using a mouse well.

Part 6 – Check what you learned (about 2 minutes)

Checklist. Put a ✓ in the box when it is true for you.

  • □ I know what a mouse is.
  • □ I know the pointer is the arrow on the screen.
  • □ I know the mouse has buttons.
  • □ I know a click is pressing a button.
  • □ I can hold the mouse gently.

When all boxes are checked, you have reviewed the main ideas from this worksheet.
