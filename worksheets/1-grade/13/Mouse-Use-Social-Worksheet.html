Mouse Use: Social Worksheet

Grade 1 · Worksheet · About 25 minutes
Work with pen and paper. Use color pencils if you like. You do not need a computer or the internet.

Name: Grade/Section: Date:
How to use this worksheet
  • Do the parts in order from top to bottom.
  • Each part has a time so you know how long to spend.
  • Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: People use the mouse to share work, play, and learn together. We take turns and take care of the mouse so everyone can use it.

Do this:

  1. Name one thing a friend can do with a mouse.
  2. Circle the fair way to share a mouse: Take turns · Grab it

Part 2 – Taking turns (about 5 minutes)

At school, one computer may have only one mouse. Friends take turns so everyone can try.

Real-life scenario: Ana and Leo want the mouse. They decide: first Ana, then Leo.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: Why is taking turns fair?

Do this: Circle Fair or Not fair.

Part 3 – Taking care (about 5 minutes)

A mouse can break if it falls or if we hit it. We care for it with clean hands and gentle clicks.

Real-life scenario: Sam drops the mouse on the floor. It stops working.

Critical thinking: How can we help our mouse last a long time?

Word bank: Gentle · Clean hands · Table

Part 4 – Helping a friend (about 5 minutes)

Sometimes a friend finds the mouse hard. We can help with kind words and patience.

Real-life scenario: Zoe cannot find the pointer. Her friend says, “Look here, I will show you.”

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: What kind words can you say to help?

Do this:

  1. Write one kind sentence for a friend.
  2. Circle the kind helper: Shows with patience · Takes the mouse away

Part 5 – Friends and the mouse (about 5 minutes)

Do this: Draw two friends at one computer sharing a mouse.

Write one thing you can do to be kind at the computer.

Part 6 – Check what you learned (about 2 minutes)

Checklist. Put a ✓ in the box when it is true for you.

  • □ I know friends take turns with a mouse.
  • □ I know to be gentle with a mouse.
  • □ I can help a friend kindly.
  • □ I know being fair makes everyone happy.
  • □ I can say one rule for sharing a computer.

When all boxes are checked, you have reviewed the main ideas from this worksheet.
