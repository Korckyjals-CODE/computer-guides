# Mouse Use – Worksheets (Grade 1, Week 13)

## For the teacher

- **Use:** Use these worksheets **whenever** the topic of mouse use fits your curriculum. They are self-contained learning activities, not tied to a specific computer project.
- **Format:** Students work **offline**, with **pen and paper** (and color pencils if you like). No computer or internet needed.
- **Time:** About **25 minutes** per worksheet. Each part has a suggested time (roughly 3–6 minutes) so students can pace themselves.
- **Guidance:** The text is written so that students can complete it **on their own**; no teacher guidance is required during the activity.
- **Standalone value:** Each worksheet teaches on its own. No computer project is required; optional hands-on activities may complement them if you choose.

## Learning objectives

### Technical worksheet

- What a **mouse** is.
- The **pointer** is the arrow on the screen.
- The mouse has **buttons**.
- A **click** is pressing a button.
- Hold the mouse gently.

### Social worksheet

- Friends take **turns** with a mouse.
- To be **gentle** with a mouse.
- **help** a friend kindly.
- Being fair makes everyone happy.
- Say one rule for sharing a computer.
