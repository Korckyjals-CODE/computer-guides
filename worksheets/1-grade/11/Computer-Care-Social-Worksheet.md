## Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

**What you will learn:** Computers at school are for **everyone**. Care means being kind to the machine and kind to classmates. In this worksheet you will think about sharing, telling a grown-up when something is wrong, and class care rules.

## Part 2 – We share the computer (about 5 minutes)

When many children use one computer, each person leaves it ready for the next person. That is **fair care**.

**Real-life scenario:** Omar finishes his turn. He leaves sticky juice on the keys and walks away. Mia sits down next. Her fingers stick. She cannot type well.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** What should Omar do before he leaves?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Circle **Yes** or **No**.

| End of turn | Kind for the next student? |
|-------------|----------------------------|
| Push the chair in and leave the computer ready | Yes · No |
| Leave crumbs on the keyboard | Yes · No |
| Tell the next child if a key feels sticky | Yes · No |

---

## Part 3 – Tell a grown-up (about 5 minutes)

If something looks broken, wet, or strange, tell a **teacher** or another grown-up. Do not hide it. Asking for help is part of care.

**Real-life scenario:** Sofia sees a crack on the tablet screen. She did not make the crack. She wonders if she should stay quiet so nobody thinks she broke it.

**Critical thinking:** Why is telling the teacher a brave and helpful choice?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Circle **Tell a grown-up** or **Keep it secret**.

| What you notice | Better choice |
|-----------------|---------------|
| A cable is almost broken | Tell a grown-up · Keep it secret |
| Water spilled near the computer | Tell a grown-up · Keep it secret |
| The screen will not turn on | Tell a grown-up · Keep it secret |

## Part 4 – Class care rules (about 5 minutes)

A short **rule** helps everyone remember how to care for shared computers.

**Real-life scenario:** Two children reach for the same mouse at once. One pulls hard. The mouse cord stretches. Both children feel upset.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** What fair rule would help next time?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Number these steps 1, 2, and 3. 1 happens first.

| Action | Order (1, 2, or 3) |
|--------|---------------------|
| The next child uses the computer gently | _____ |
| Children agree who goes first | _____ |
| The first child finishes and leaves it ready | _____ |
