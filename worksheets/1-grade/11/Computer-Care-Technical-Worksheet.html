Computer Care: Technical Worksheet

Grade 1 · Worksheet · About 25 minutes
Work with pen and paper. Use color pencils if you like. You do not need a computer or the internet.

Name: Grade/Section: Date:
How to use this worksheet
  • Do the parts in order from top to bottom.
  • Each part has a time so you know how long to spend.
  • Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: Computer care means keeping a computer clean, dry, and safe so it can keep working. In this worksheet you will name gentle ways to touch a computer, keep drinks away, and carry it carefully.

Do this:

  1. Have you used a computer or tablet at school? Circle: Yes or No
  2. Name one thing you already do to be careful with it.

Part 2 – Gentle hands (about 5 minutes)

A computer has a screen, keys, and sometimes a mouse. Soft touches keep parts from breaking. Hard taps, poking the screen with a pencil, or slamming the lid can hurt the machine.

Real-life scenario: Maya wants the screen to wake up. She taps lightly with one finger. Sam taps hard with a crayon. Maya's computer wakes up. Sam's screen gets a dark mark.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: What is a gentle way to wake a screen?

Do this: Circle Gentle or Not gentle.

Action Gentle or Not gentle?
Press keys softly Gentle · Not gentle
Poke the screen with a pencil tip Gentle · Not gentle
Close a laptop lid slowly Gentle · Not gentle
Slam the lid shut Gentle · Not gentle

Part 3 – Keep it dry (about 5 minutes)

Liquids (water, juice, milk) can go inside a computer and stop it from working. Keep drinks away from the keyboard and the screen.

Real-life scenario: Leo brings a juice box to the computer table. He puts it next to the keyboard. When he reaches for a pencil, the juice tips over.

Critical thinking: Where should Leo put the juice instead? Write one place.

Do this: Write Safe or Not safe.

Where the drink is Safe or Not safe?
On a side table away from the computer
On top of the keyboard
In a backpack across the room

Part 4 – Carry with care (about 5 minutes)

When you move a laptop or tablet, use two hands. Walk slowly. Do not swing it or set it on the edge of a desk.

Real-life scenario: The teacher asks Ana to bring a tablet to the reading corner. Ana holds it with both hands and walks. Ben carries one with one hand and runs.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: Who is taking better care of the tablet? Why?

Do this:

  1. Circle the safer way: Two hands and walk · One hand and run
  2. Write one sentence. What could happen if a tablet falls?

Part 5 – Draw a care tip (about 5 minutes)

Do this:

  1. Draw a computer or tablet being cared for. Show one care tip (gentle hands, no drinks, or two-hand carry).
  1. Write your tip in a few words under your drawing.

Part 6 – Check what you learned (about 2 minutes)

Checklist. Put a ✓ in the box when it is true for you.

  • □ I know computer care means keeping a computer safe so it can keep working.
  • □ I can tell a gentle touch from a hard one.
  • □ I know to keep drinks away from computers.
  • □ I know to carry a laptop or tablet with two hands.
  • □ I can draw or say one care tip.

When all boxes are checked, you have reviewed the main ideas from this worksheet.
