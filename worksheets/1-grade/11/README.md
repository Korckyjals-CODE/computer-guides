# Computer Care – Worksheets (Grade 1, Week 11)

## For the teacher

- **Use:** Use these worksheets **whenever** the topic of computer care fits your curriculum. They are self-contained learning activities, not tied to a specific computer project.
- **Format:** Students work **offline**, with **pen and paper** (and color pencils if you like). No computer or internet needed.
- **Time:** About **25 minutes** per worksheet. Each part has a suggested time (roughly 3–6 minutes) so students can pace themselves. Sentences and tasks are kept short for grade 1.
- **Guidance:** The text is written so that students can complete it **on their own**; no teacher guidance is required during the activity.
- **Standalone value:** Each worksheet teaches on its own. No computer project is required; optional hands-on activities may complement them if you choose.

## Learning objectives

### Technical worksheet

- Explain that **computer care** keeps a machine working.
- Tell a **gentle** touch from a hard one.
- Keep **drinks** away from computers.
- Carry a laptop or tablet with **two hands**.
- Draw or state one care tip.

### Social worksheet

- Recognize that school computers are for the **whole class**.
- Leave a computer **ready** for the next student.
- **Tell a grown-up** when something looks wrong.
- Suggest a **fair rule** for sharing.
- Make a short care poster.
