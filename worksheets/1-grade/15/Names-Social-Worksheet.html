Names: Social Worksheet

Grade 1 · Worksheet · About 25 minutes
Work with pen and paper. Use color pencils if you like. You do not need a computer or the internet.

Name: Grade/Section: Date:
How to use this worksheet
  • Do the parts in order from top to bottom.
  • Each part has a time so you know how long to spend.
  • Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: Names are important to people. Using each person's name with kindness shows respect and helps us work together.

Do this:

  1. Write the name of one classmate you like to work with.
  2. Circle the kind way to call a friend: Use the name · Shout hey

Part 2 – Names of people (about 5 minutes)

Every person has a name. When we say a name correctly, the person feels respected.

Real-life scenario: A new student joins the class. Nobody asks for her name.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: How can the class welcome her?

Do this: Circle Kind or Not kind.

What we doKind?
Ask her nameKind · Not kind
Call her “you”Kind · Not kind
Say her name correctlyKind · Not kind
Make fun of her nameKind · Not kind

Part 3 – Names on shared work (about 5 minutes)

When friends share work, they write their names on it. Then everyone knows who made it.

Real-life scenario: Two drawings have no names. Both friends say they made the same one.

Critical thinking: How could names fix this?

Word bank: Name · Friend · Drawing

Do this: Fill in the best word.

SentenceWord
I write my ... on my work
Each ... makes a drawing
We put the name on the ...

Part 4 – Kind names (about 5 minutes)

Some words hurt. We use kind names and do not give mean nicknames.

Real-life scenario: A child is called a mean nickname at the lab.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: What can you say to help?

Do this:

  1. Write one kind thing to say.
  2. Circle the kind choice: Use real names · Mean nicknames

Part 5 – Name tag (about 5 minutes)

Do this: Draw a name tag with your name and a small drawing.

Write why names are important.

Part 6 – Check what you learned (about 2 minutes)

Checklist. Put a ✓ in the box when it is true for you.

  • □ I know people have names.
  • □ I can say a name kindly.
  • □ I put my name on my work.
  • □ I do not use mean names.
  • □ I can welcome a new friend.

When all boxes are checked, you have reviewed the main ideas from this worksheet.
