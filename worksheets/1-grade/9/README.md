# Machines Help – Worksheets (Grade 1, Week 9)

## For the teacher

- **Use:** Use these worksheets **whenever** the topic of machines that help people fits your curriculum. They are self-contained learning activities, not tied to a specific computer project.
- **Format:** Students work **offline**, with **pen and paper** (and color pencils if you like). No computer or internet needed.
- **Time:** About **25 minutes** per worksheet. Each part has a suggested time (roughly 3–6 minutes) so students can pace themselves. Sentences and tasks are kept short for grade 1.
- **Guidance:** The text is written so that students can complete it **on their own**; no teacher guidance is required during the activity.
- **Standalone value:** Each worksheet teaches on its own. No computer project is required; optional hands-on activities may complement them if you choose.

## Learning objectives

### Technical worksheet

- Name a **machine** and the **job** it helps a person do.
- Point to a visible **part**, such as a button, handle, or door.
- Tell a **start** from a **stop**.
- Notice that a machine can repeat a job while a person still checks it.
- Draw a classroom helper and label its part and its job.

### Social worksheet

- Say who a machine helps and who still needs a person.
- Choose a safer action around machines that spin, move, or get hot.
- Write a **fair rule** for one shared classroom machine.
- Put turn-taking steps in order.
- Make a short sign that states the rule.
