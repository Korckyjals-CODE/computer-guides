## Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

**What you will learn:** School keyboards are for the **whole class**. Kind keyboard habits mean taking turns, keeping food away, and leaving the keys ready for the next student.

## Part 2 – Taking turns at the keyboard (about 5 minutes)

When two children want the same keyboard, **waiting** and **asking** keeps everyone calm. Grabbing keys out of someone's hands is not fair.

**Real-life scenario:** Diego is typing his name. Ava pulls the keyboard toward herself. Diego's fingers slip. Both children feel upset.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** What kind words could Ava say instead of pulling?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Circle **Yes** or **No**.

| Action | Kind? Circle |
|---|---|
| Ask, “May I type when you finish?” | Yes · No |
| Yank the keyboard away | Yes · No |
| Wait quietly for a turn | Yes · No |

## Part 3 – Keep the keyboard ready (about 5 minutes)

Crumbs, sticky fingers, and toys on the keys make typing hard for the next person. Leaving the keyboard **clean and clear** is part of sharing.

**Real-life scenario:** Noah finishes his turn with cracker crumbs on the keys. Sofia sits down. Her fingers feel sticky and she cannot find the letter S.

**Critical thinking:** What should Noah do before he leaves the computer?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Circle **Yes** or **No**.

| End of turn | Ready for next student? |
|---|---|
| Brush crumbs away (or tell a teacher) | Yes · No |
| Leave a juice box on the keys | Yes · No |
| Push the chair in gently | Yes · No |

---

## Part 4 – Tell a grown-up (about 5 minutes)

If a key is stuck, missing, or wet, tell a **teacher**. Asking for help protects the keyboard for everyone.

**Real-life scenario:** Emma sees water near the keyboard. She did not spill it. She wonders if she should stay quiet.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Why is telling the teacher a helpful choice?

_________________________________________

**Do this:**

1. **Circle** the better choice if a key will not work: Tell a grown-up · Hide it and walk away

2. **Write who** you would tell at school.
