## How to use this worksheet

- Do the parts **in order** from top to bottom.
- Each part has a **time** so you know how long to spend.
- Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

---

## Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

**What you will learn:** People share and care for files together. We save our work, keep it tidy, and ask before we open or change a friend's files.

**Do this:**

1. **Name** one file you would like to share with a friend.

   _________________________

2. **Circle** the kind thing to do with a friend's file: Ask first · Delete it

## Part 2 – Ask first (about 5 minutes)

A file made by a friend belongs to that friend. We ask before we open or change it.

**Real-life scenario:** Sam sees Ana's drawing file. He asks, “May I look?”

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Why do we ask first?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Circle **Kind** or **Not kind**.

| What happens | Kind? |
|---|---|
| We ask before opening | Kind · Not kind |
| We delete a friend's file | Kind · Not kind |
| We say thank you | Kind · Not kind |
| We change it without asking | Kind · Not kind |

## Part 3 – Tidy folders (about 5 minutes)

A tidy folder helps everyone find files quickly. We put files back where they belong.

Sharing a file lets friends see your work. We share only with people we trust.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Who can you share a file with?
