# System Issues – Worksheets (Grade 8, Week 11)

## For the teacher

- **Use:** Use these worksheets **whenever** system issues fit your curriculum. They are self-contained; not tied to a specific computer project.
- **Format:** Offline, pen and paper (color pencils optional). No computer or internet needed.
- **Time:** About **25 minutes** per worksheet.
- **Guidance:** Students can complete the activities on their own.
- **Standalone value:** Each worksheet teaches on its own.

## Learning objectives

### Technical
- Explain what a **system issue** is and classify hardware, software, network, and configuration cases.
- Use symptoms, evidence, and **isolation** questions.
- Decide when to restart, document, or escalate.
- Sketch a basic diagnosis flow.

### Social
- Recognize **uneven** human impact during outages.
- Prefer **learning** responses over blame without evidence.
- Draft clear, honest communication during failures.
- Design a group response board that includes people.
