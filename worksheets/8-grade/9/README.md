# Smart Devices – Worksheets (Grade 8, Week 9)

## For the teacher

- **Use:** Use these worksheets **whenever** the topic of smart devices fits your curriculum. They are self-contained learning activities, not tied to a specific computer project.
- **Format:** Students work **offline**, with **pen and paper** (and color pencils if you like). No computer or internet needed.
- **Time:** About **25 minutes** per worksheet. Each part has a suggested time (roughly 3–6 minutes) so students can pace themselves.
- **Guidance:** The text is written so that students can complete it **on their own**; no teacher guidance is required during the activity.
- **Standalone value:** Each worksheet teaches on its own. No computer project is required; optional hands-on activities may complement them if you choose.

## Learning objectives

### Technical worksheet

- Describe a **smart device** as sensor, processor, connection, and action working together.
- Distinguish a decision made **on the device** from a decision made **in the cloud**.
- Decide which jobs must keep working when the network fails, using a lock and a thermostat as cases.
- Identify data a device creates and whether that data must leave the home for the main job.
- Sketch what stays in the home and what crosses the connection.

### Social worksheet

- Apply **consent** to a shared smart speaker, including guests who did not choose the device.
- Explain **single point of control** when one phone owns the lock, thermostat, or lights.
- Analyze the ongoing cost of a cheap device that depends on an account or a company service.
- Compare a normal product with a connected one on outages, shutdowns, and sharing.
- Write a house rule and name who that rule might still leave out.
