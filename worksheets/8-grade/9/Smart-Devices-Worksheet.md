## Part 2 – What is a smart device? (about 5 minutes)

A **smart device** is an electronic tool that can collect information, make decisions, and communicate with other devices or systems. It often includes a processor, sensors, and some form of connectivity. Examples include smart speakers, smart watches, smart lights, and connected thermostats.

## Part 3 – How smart devices work (about 6 minutes)

Smart devices usually contain a few important parts:

- **Sensors** detect information such as motion, temperature, or light.
- **Processing** helps the device understand that information.
- **Connection** allows the device to communicate with apps, networks, or other devices.
- **Action** lets the device respond by showing information, turning on lights, sending alerts, or adjusting settings.

This makes the device more responsive and helpful.

## Part 4 – Everyday uses of smart devices (about 5 minutes)

Smart devices are common in daily life. They can help people with health tracking, home control, safety, communication, and automation. For example, a smart speaker can play music, a smart lock can check who is entering, and a smartwatch can track activity or send reminders.
