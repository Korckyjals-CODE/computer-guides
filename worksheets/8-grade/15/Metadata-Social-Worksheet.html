Metadata: Social Worksheet

Grade 8 · Worksheet · About 25 minutes
Work with pen and paper. Use color pencils if you like. You do not need a computer or the internet.

Name: Grade/Section: Date:
How to use this worksheet
  • Do the parts in order from top to bottom.
  • Each part has a time so you know how long to spend.
  • Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: Metadata can reveal personal information such as location and time. Sharing files without checking metadata can affect privacy and safety.

Do this:

  1. Name one piece of metadata that could reveal where you were.
  2. Circle what to check before sharing: Photo metadata · The wallpaper

Part 2 – Hidden details (about 5 minutes)

A photo can store GPS location and time. Posting it online may show where you live or study.

Real-life scenario: A student posts a photo from home. Others use its location tag to find the address.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: What could she do before sharing?

Part 3 – Consent and metadata (about 5 minutes)

Before sharing a photo of others, ask for consent. Their names or tags are metadata too.

Real-life scenario: A teacher tags every student's full name in a public album.

Critical thinking: What concern does this raise?

Word bank: consent · privacy · tag

Part 4 – Using metadata well (about 5 minutes)

Good metadata can also help: captions with alternative text help people who cannot see images, and credits respect authors.

Real-life scenario: A designer adds a credit and description to her poster.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: Why is this helpful?

Do this:

  1. Write a good caption for a school event photo.
  2. Circle the responsible habit: Credit the author · Remove the author's name
