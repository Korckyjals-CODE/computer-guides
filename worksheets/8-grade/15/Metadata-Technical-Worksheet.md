## Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

**What you will learn:** **Metadata** is data that describes other data. A photo is the data; its **date**, **size**, **camera** and **location** are metadata. Metadata makes files easy to search, sort, and manage.

**Do this:**

1. **Name** one detail your phone stores about a photo.

   _________________________

2. **Circle** what metadata is: Data about data · A kind of virus

## Part 2 – Types of metadata (about 5 minutes)

Common metadata: **descriptive** (title, author), **technical** (size, format), and **administrative** (date, permissions). Files carry it automatically or you add it.

**Real-life scenario:** A photo shows 2.4 MB, JPG, taken on 12 Nov, and created by Ana.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Which details are technical and which are descriptive?

_________________________________________

| Detail | Type |
|---|---|
| File size | Technical · Descriptive |
| Title | Technical · Descriptive |
| Format | Technical · Descriptive |
| Author | Technical · Descriptive |

## Part 3 – How we use metadata (about 5 minutes)

Search engines, libraries and music apps use metadata. Without **tags** and titles, finding a song among thousands would be very slow.

**Real-life scenario:** A music app shows songs by artist, album and year.

**Critical thinking:** What metadata did the app use?

## Part 4 – Viewing and editing (about 5 minutes)

In most systems you can open **Properties** or **Details** to see metadata. You can edit titles and tags but not always size.
