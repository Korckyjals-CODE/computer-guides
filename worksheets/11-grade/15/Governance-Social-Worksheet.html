Governance: Social Worksheet

Grade 11 · Worksheet · About 25 minutes
Work with pen and paper. Use color pencils if you like. You do not need a computer or the internet.

Name: Grade/Section: Date:
How to use this worksheet
  • Do the parts in order from top to bottom.
  • Each part has a time so you know how long to spend.
  • Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: Governance affects people. Good governance is transparent, fair and participatory. Poor governance can lead to abuse of power and loss of trust.

Do this:

  1. Name one decision where people should have a voice.
  2. Circle a sign of good governance: Transparency · Secrecy

Part 2 – Transparency and trust (about 5 minutes)

People trust organizations that explain decisions, publish policies and report results.

Real-life scenario: A company changes how it uses customer data without telling anyone.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: How might customers feel?

Do this: Circle Transparent or Not transparent.

Part 3 – Stakeholders (about 5 minutes)

Stakeholders are people affected by decisions: students, parents, staff and community. Good governance listens to them.

Real-life scenario: A school bans phones without consulting students or parents.

Critical thinking: Who should have been asked?

Word bank: listen · represent · include

Do this: Fill in the best word.

SentenceWord
Governance should ... stakeholders
Voices must be ...
Groups need to be ...

Part 4 – Ethics and conflict of interest (about 5 minutes)

A conflict of interest happens when a decision-maker benefits personally. Governance requires disclosure and fair procedures.

Real-life scenario: A manager chooses his cousin's company for a contract.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: What should have happened?

Do this:

  1. Write one rule to prevent conflicts of interest.
  2. Circle the better practice: Disclose and step aside · Hide the relationship

Part 5 – Trust charter (about 5 minutes)

Do this: Draw a charter with three promises for fair governance.

Choose the promise you value most.

Part 6 – Check what you learned (about 2 minutes)

Checklist. Put a ✓ in the box when it is true for you.

  • □ I know good governance is transparent.
  • □ I can name stakeholders.
  • □ I understand conflict of interest.
  • □ I value fairness.
  • □ I can write a charter.

When all boxes are checked, you have reviewed the main ideas from this worksheet.
