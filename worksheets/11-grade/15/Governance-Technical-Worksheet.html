Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: Governance is the system of policies, roles, decisions and controls that guides how an organization uses its resources. In IT, governance ensures that technology supports goals, manages risk and follows rules.

Do this:

  1. Name one rule at school that comes from a policy.
  2. Circle what governance sets: Who decides and how · Which color to use

Part 2 – Elements of governance (about 5 minutes)

Key elements: policies (rules), roles (who is responsible), processes (how decisions are made), and controls (checks such as audits and logs).

Real-life scenario: A school wants to buy new tablets. A committee approves the budget and a policy lists acceptable use.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: Which element is the committee? Which is the policy?

Part 3 – Frameworks (about 5 minutes)

Organizations use frameworks such as COBIT or ISO 27001. A framework offers proven practices so that each organization does not start from zero.

Real-life scenario: A company adopts a security framework and then trains staff.

Critical thinking: Why use an existing framework?

Word bank: risk · compliance · value

Do this: Match the goal to its meaning.

GoalMeaning
Reduce harm
Follow laws
Deliver benefits

Part 4 – Accountability (about 5 minutes)

Accountability means a named person answers for results. RACI charts show who is Responsible, Accountable, Consulted and Informed.

Real-life scenario: A system fails and each manager blames another team.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: How would a RACI chart help?

Do this:

  1. Write who should be accountable for a school's student database and why.
  2. Circle the clearer structure: One accountable owner · Everyone and no one

Part 5 – Governance map (about 5 minutes)

Do this: Draw a diagram with policy, roles, process and controls connected.

Explain how they work together.
