## Part 2 – Neutral posture checklist (about 5 minutes)

Targets: feet supported, hips/knees ~comfortable, screen near eye height, elbows ~open angle, wrists neutral—not bent sharply up/down.

**Real-life scenario:** A laptop on a low desk makes a student crane their neck for hours. A simple riser and external keyboard change the angles.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Which change helps more first: screen height or chair height? Why might both matter?

## Part 3 – Input devices and microbreaks (about 5 minutes)

Mouse position near the body’s midline, light key force, and **microbreaks** (20–30s every ~20–30 min) reduce cumulative strain. Stretching beats ignoring early pain.

**Real-life scenario:** After gaming with a low mouse sensitivity requiring big arm sweeps, a student feels shoulder burn.

**Critical thinking:** How could settings or device placement reduce that motion?

## Part 4 – Vision and lighting (about 5 minutes)

Reduce glare, match brightness to the room, increase font size when squinting, and use the 20-20-20 idea (every 20 minutes look 20 feet away for 20 seconds) as a practical heuristic.

**Real-life scenario:** A window behind a monitor creates glare; the student raises brightness to max and gets headaches.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** What environmental fix beats maxing brightness?

_________________________________________
