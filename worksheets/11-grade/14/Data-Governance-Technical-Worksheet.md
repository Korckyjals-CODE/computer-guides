## Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

**What you will learn:** **Data governance** is the set of **policies**, **roles**, and **processes** that keep an organization's data **accurate**, **secure**, and **used properly**. Key roles: **data owner**, **data steward**, and **data custodian**.

## Part 2 – Roles (about 5 minutes)

A **data owner** is accountable for a data set. A **steward** looks after quality and rules. A **custodian** keeps it safe technically.

**Real-life scenario:** A hospital names a steward for patient records.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Why assign clear roles?

| Checks accuracy and definitions | Owner · Steward · Custodian |
| Runs backups and security | Owner · Steward · Custodian |

## Part 3 – Policies and standards (about 5 minutes)

**Policies** state what must be done. **Standards** define how, for example date formats or naming rules.

**Real-life scenario:** A company has a policy that all personal data must be encrypted.

**Critical thinking:** How does a standard support a policy?
