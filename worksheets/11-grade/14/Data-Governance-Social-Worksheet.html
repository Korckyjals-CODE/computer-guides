Data Governance: Social Worksheet

Grade 11 · Worksheet · About 25 minutes
Work with pen and paper. Use color pencils if you like. You do not need a computer or the internet.

Name: Grade/Section: Date:
How to use this worksheet
  • Do the parts in order from top to bottom.
  • Each part has a time so you know how long to spend.
  • Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: Governance is about trust and fairness. Good governance protects people's rights, avoids bias, and holds organizations accountable.

Do this:

  1. Name a group that could be harmed by misused data.
  2. Circle the accountable practice: Audit and report openly · Hide mistakes

Part 2 – Accountability (about 5 minutes)

Organizations must be accountable: they explain how data is used and fix problems.

Real-life scenario: A bank's data is used in an unfair loan decision.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: Who should be accountable?

Do this: Circle Accountable or Not accountable.

ActionAccountable?
Publish audit resultsAccountable · Not accountable
Blame the computerAccountable · Not accountable
Fix the unfair ruleAccountable · Not accountable
Ignore complaintsAccountable · Not accountable

Part 3 – Fairness and bias (about 5 minutes)

Biased or missing data can lead to unfair decisions.

Real-life scenario: A data set has few examples from rural students.

Critical thinking: What could go wrong?

Word bank: Bias · Fair · Inclusive

Do this: Write the best word.

SituationWord
Missing groups cause ...
Good data is ...
Data should be ...

Part 4 – Ethical culture (about 5 minutes)

Policies work only if people follow them. Training and leadership build a culture of care.

Real-life scenario: A team trains staff yearly on privacy.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: Why is culture as important as rules?

Do this:

  1. Write one way to build a data-responsible culture.
  2. Circle the stronger approach: Training and leadership · Rules only on paper

Part 5 – Trust poster (about 5 minutes)

Do this: Draw a poster with three promises an organization makes about data.

Write which promise matters most.

Part 6 – Check what you learned (about 2 minutes)

Checklist. Put a ✓ in the box when it is true for you.

  • □ I value accountability.
  • □ I notice bias.
  • □ I support inclusive data.
  • □ I help build culture.
  • □ I respect people's rights.

When all boxes are checked, you have reviewed the main ideas from this worksheet.
