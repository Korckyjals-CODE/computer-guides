Data Governance: Technical Worksheet

Work with pen and paper. Use color pencils if you like. You do not need a computer or the internet.

How to use this worksheet
  • Do the parts in order from top to bottom.

Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: Data governance is the set of policies, roles, and processes that keep an organization's data accurate, secure, and used properly. Key roles: data owner, data steward, and data custodian.

Do this:

  1. Name one rule an organization might have about data.
  2. Circle the person responsible for a data set's quality: Data steward · Visitor

Part 2 – Roles (about 5 minutes)

A data owner is accountable for a data set. A steward looks after quality and rules. A custodian keeps it safe technically.

Real-life scenario: A hospital names a steward for patient records.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: Why assign clear roles?

Do this: Match each task with a role. Circle one.

Decides who may use the dataOwner · Steward · Custodian
Checks accuracy and definitionsOwner · Steward · Custodian

Part 3 – Policies and standards (about 5 minutes)

Policies state what must be done. Standards define how, for example date formats or naming rules.

Real-life scenario: A company has a policy that all personal data must be encrypted.

Critical thinking: How does a standard support a policy?

Word bank: Policy · Standard · Compliance

Do this: Write the term that matches.

DescriptionTerm
What must be done
How to do it
Following the rules

Part 4 – Compliance and risk (about 5 minutes)

Laws like GDPR and local privacy laws require protecting personal data. Breaking them can cause fines and lost trust.

Real-life scenario: A company loses customer data and did not report it.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: What risks does it face?

Do this:

  1. Write two risks of poor data governance.
  2. Circle the stronger governance: Written policies and audits · No rules

Part 5 – Governance map (about 5 minutes)

Do this: Draw a chart linking a policy, three roles, and one standard.

Write which role you would take and why.
