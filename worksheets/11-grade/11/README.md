# Risk Management – Worksheets (Grade 11, Week 11)

## For the teacher

- **Use:** Whenever risk management fits your curriculum. Self-contained; not tied to a computer project.
- **Format:** Offline, pen and paper. About **25 minutes** each.
- **Guidance:** Students can work independently.
- **Standalone value:** Each worksheet teaches on its own.

## Files

- **Risk-Management-Technical-Worksheet.html** / **.md**

### Social
- **Risk-Management-Social-Worksheet.html** / **.md**

## Learning objectives

### Technical
- Identify risks as asset–threat–consequence statements.
- Separate likelihood from impact; prioritize treatments.
- Choose mitigate / transfer / avoid / accept deliberately.
- Sketch a mini risk register.

### Social
- Ask whose harms count in risk decisions.
- Contrast org-centered vs people-centered impact framing.
- Practice honest risk communication; weigh ethical trade-offs.
- Outline a stakeholder-aware risk decision board.
