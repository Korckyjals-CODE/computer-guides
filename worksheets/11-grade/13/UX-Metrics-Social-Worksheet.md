## Part 2 – Privacy and data (about 5 minutes)

Data about clicks, location, and time can reveal personal habits. Collect only what is needed and keep it secure.

**Real-life scenario:** An app tracks location even when you are not using it.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** What could go wrong if this data leaks?

## Part 3 – Goodhart's Law (about 5 minutes)

When a measure becomes a target, it stops being a good measure. Chasing numbers can lead to bad behavior.

**Real-life scenario:** A support team is rewarded for short calls, so agents hang up early.

**Critical thinking:** How could a better metric include real help?

_________________________________________

**Word bank:** *Quality* · *Wellbeing* · *Fairness* · *Honesty*

**Do this:** Add a value that metrics should reflect.

| Situation | Value |
|---|---|
| Short calls but unsolved issues | _____ |
| Time-on-app up but sleep down | _____ |
| Metrics ignore some user groups | _____ |

## Part 4 – Fair samples (about 5 minutes)

Metrics from a narrow group may not represent everyone. Include different users and report uncertainty.

**Real-life scenario:** A test uses only fast internet users. Slow-connection users struggle.
