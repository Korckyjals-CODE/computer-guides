## Part 2 – Categories and formats (about 5 minutes)

Group related data into **categories**. Use the same **format** each time, for example dates as 2026-11-20.

**Real-life scenario:** A list has dates written as 11/20, 20 Nov, and Nov 20th.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** What problem could that cause?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Decide which format is consistent. Circle the best.

| Entries | Consistent? |
|---|---|
| 2026-11-20, 2026-11-21 | Yes · No |
| 20 Nov, 11/21 | Yes · No |
| Nov 20th, 21/11/2026 | Yes · No |

## Part 3 – Metadata (about 5 minutes)

**Metadata** describes a file: author, date, size, and type. It helps you search and sort.

**Real-life scenario:** A photo's metadata shows the date and place it was taken.

**Critical thinking:** How can metadata help find a file?
