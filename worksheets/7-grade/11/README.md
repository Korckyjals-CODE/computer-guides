# Preventive Care – Worksheets (Grade 7, Week 11)

## For the teacher

- **Use:** Use these worksheets **whenever** preventive care fits your curriculum. They are self-contained; not tied to a specific computer project.
- **Format:** Offline, pen and paper (color pencils optional). No computer or internet needed.
- **Time:** About **25 minutes** per worksheet.
- **Guidance:** Students can complete the activities on their own.
- **Standalone value:** Each worksheet teaches on its own.

## Files

- **Preventive-Care-Technical-Worksheet.html** / **.md**

### Social

## Learning objectives

### Technical
- Distinguish **preventive** from **reactive** care.
- Link updates, backups, and storage habits to reliability.
- Plan physical and scheduled checks.
- Sketch a preventive plan for one device.

### Social
- Treat preventive care as a **shared** responsibility.
- Practice **early reporting** and resist pressure to skip care steps.
- Propose fair rules for maintenance downtime.
- Draft a community care pledge.
