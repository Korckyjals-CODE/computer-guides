## Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

**What you will learn:** **Preventive care** means taking steps *before* a system fails—updates, backups, cleaning, and scheduled checks. It is different from **reactive care**, which starts after something already broke. In this worksheet you will compare those approaches and plan simple preventive actions.

## Part 2 – Preventive vs reactive (about 5 minutes)

**Preventive** actions reduce the chance of failure. **Reactive** actions respond after symptoms appear. Both matter, but preventive care often costs less time and stress when planned well.

**Real-life scenario:** A school laptop cart skips updates for months. Suddenly many devices will not open the learning app on exam week. Another cart updates monthly and keeps a spare charger set ready.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Which cart used more preventive care? What reactive work will the first cart need now?

## Part 3 – Updates, backups, and space (about 5 minutes)

**Updates** patch bugs and security holes. **Backups** keep copies of important files somewhere separate. Watching **storage space** and battery health helps you spot slowdowns early. Ignoring warnings is like ignoring a car's oil light.

**Real-life scenario:** Priya sees “storage almost full” for two weeks. She keeps downloading large videos. Then she cannot save her science report the night before it is due.

**Critical thinking:** Name one preventive step Priya could have taken when the warning first appeared.

## Part 4 – Physical and scheduled checks (about 5 minutes)

Dust, loose cables, blocked vents, and frayed cords are physical risks. A **checklist on a calendar** (weekly, monthly) turns care into a routine instead of a scramble.

**Real-life scenario:** A classroom desktop overheats because vents are packed with dust. Students notice it shuts down during long videos. Nobody had a monthly dust-check habit.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Why does a schedule help more than “we'll clean it when it feels slow”?
