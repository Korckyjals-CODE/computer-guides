System Naming: Social Worksheet

Grade 7 · Worksheet · About 25 minutes
Work with pen and paper. Use color pencils if you like. You do not need a computer or the internet.

Name: Grade/Section: Date:
How to use this worksheet
  • Do the parts in order from top to bottom.
  • Each part has a time so you know how long to spend.
  • Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: System names affect real people. Usernames, email addresses and device names reveal information, so choosing them responsibly protects privacy and shows respect.

Do this:

  1. Name one detail a username should not reveal.
  2. Circle safer username: A nickname without personal data · Full name and birthday

Part 2 – Usernames and privacy (about 5 minutes)

A username with your full name and birth year can help a stranger guess your password or find you.

Real-life scenario: A student uses “JuanPerez2012” for every site.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: What risk does this create?

Do this: Circle Safer or Riskier.

Part 3 – Respectful names (about 5 minutes)

Names that appear on shared screens or logs should be appropriate for everyone. Offensive names can hurt others and lead to sanctions.

Real-life scenario: A student names a school computer with an insulting word.

Critical thinking: What could happen and why does it matter?

Word bank: respect · rules · consequence

Do this: Fill in the best word.

SentenceWord
We use ... in public names
The school has ...
A ... can follow offensive names

Part 4 – Inclusive naming (about 5 minutes)

Systems should allow names with accents, ñ and other languages. If not, people feel excluded.

Real-life scenario: A form rejects the name “Núñez” because of the ñ.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: How does this affect the user?

Do this:

  1. Write one change a system could make to include more names.
  2. Circle the inclusive design: Accept accents · Reject unusual letters

Part 5 – Naming guide (about 5 minutes)

Do this: Draw a poster with three tips for choosing safe, respectful names.

Choose the most important tip.

Part 6 – Check what you learned (about 2 minutes)

Checklist. Put a ✓ in the box when it is true for you.

  • □ I protect privacy in usernames.
  • □ I use respectful names.
  • □ I understand inclusive naming.
  • □ I can give three naming tips.
  • □ I can explain a risk.
