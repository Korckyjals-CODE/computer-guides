Interaction Design: Social Worksheet

Grade 7 · Worksheet · About 25 minutes
Work with pen and paper. Use color pencils if you like. You do not need a computer or the internet.

Name: Grade/Section: Date:
How to use this worksheet
  • Do the parts in order from top to bottom.
  • Each part has a time so you know how long to spend.
  • Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: Design shapes behavior. Digital products can help or manipulate people. Ethical interaction design respects users' time, attention, privacy, and choices.

Do this:

  1. Write one app that makes it hard to stop using it.
  2. Circle the ethical choice: Respect user attention · Trick users to stay longer

Part 2 – Design that helps (about 5 minutes)

Helpful designs are clear, honest, and respect the user's goals. They make it easy to cancel, pause, or undo.

Real-life scenario: A video app asks “Take a break?” after two hours.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: How can design help people use technology in healthy ways?

Do this: Circle Respectful or Manipulative.

Part 3 – Inclusive interaction (about 5 minutes)

People use different languages, abilities, and devices. Inclusive design offers alternatives, like captions and voice control.

Real-life scenario: A video has captions so deaf students and noisy-room viewers can follow.

Critical thinking: Who else benefits from captions?

Word bank: Captions · Large text · Voice control · Keyboard-only use

Do this: Match a feature to a need.

NeedFeature

Cannot use a mouse
Has trouble reading small text

Part 4 – Privacy and consent (about 5 minutes)

Good interactions ask permission clearly. Users should know what data is collected and why.

Real-life scenario: An app asks to use your microphone with a short, clear explanation.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: Why should permissions be explained in plain words?

Do this:

  1. Write one question you should ask before allowing an app to use your camera.
  2. Circle the respectful design: Clear permission request · Pre-checked hidden boxes

Part 5 – Ethical design poster (about 5 minutes)

Do this: Draw a poster with two rules for respectful design.

Write one rule you wish every app followed.

Part 6 – Check what you learned (about 2 minutes)

Checklist. Put a ✓ in the box when it is true for you.

  • □ I can describe respectful vs. manipulative design.
  • □ I know inclusive features.
  • □ I know why consent matters.
  • □ I can question persuasive design.
  • □ I can state a rule for ethical design.

When all boxes are checked, you have reviewed the main ideas from this worksheet.
