# Reliability – Worksheets (Grade 9, Week 11)

## For the teacher

- **Use:** Whenever reliability fits your curriculum. Self-contained; not tied to a computer project.
- **Format:** Offline, pen and paper. About **25 minutes** each.
- **Guidance:** Students can work independently.
- **Standalone value:** Each worksheet teaches on its own.

## Files

### Technical
- **Reliability-Technical-Worksheet.html** / **.md**

### Social
- **Reliability-Social-Worksheet.html** / **.md**

## Learning objectives

### Technical
- Explain reliability, uptime, and why timing of downtime matters.
- Contrast redundancy with single points of failure.
- List common failure modes and simple prevention steps.
- Sketch a more reliable setup with a backup path.

### Social
- Connect reliability to trust and everyday dependence.
- Recognize unequal impact of outages and suggest fair responses.
- Practice calm, honest status communication.
- Outline community habits that support dependable systems.
