## Part 2 – Labels, affordances, feedback (about 5 minutes)

Every field needs a clear **label**, a visible **affordance** (it looks typeable/clickable), and **feedback** (success, error, progress). Silence after submit confuses users.

**Real-life scenario:** A “Join club” button does nothing visible for 8 seconds while the server works. Users click five more times and create duplicates.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** What feedback should appear immediately?

## Part 3 – Constraints and defaults (about 5 minutes)

**Constraints** guide valid input (date pickers, numeric steppers). Sensible **defaults** speed experts but must not trick beginners into wrong choices.

**Real-life scenario:** A permission checkbox is pre-checked to share location. Many teens miss it and share unintentionally.

## Part 4 – Progressive disclosure (about 5 minutes)

Show only needed fields first; reveal advanced options later. Overwhelming first screens raise abandonment.

**Real-life scenario:** A student account form asks for 25 fields including rarely used admin codes on page one. Half the class quits.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Which fields belong on step 1 vs later?
