## Part 2 – Privacy and control (about 5 minutes)

When data is in the cloud, a company holds it. Strong passwords and encryption protect it.

**Real-life scenario:** A student uploads personal photos without a password.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** What could happen?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Circle **Safe** or **Risky**.

| Action | Safe or risky? |
|---|---|
| Strong password and two-step login | Safe · Risky |
| Sharing a password | Safe · Risky |
| Encrypting private files | Safe · Risky |
| Public link to private files | Safe · Risky |

## Part 4 – Digital footprint and waste (about 5 minutes)

Data centers use energy, and old drives become e-waste. We can delete what we do not need and recycle devices.

**Real-life scenario:** A student keeps 10,000 old photos on three drives.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** What is a responsible choice?

_________________________________________

**Do this:**
