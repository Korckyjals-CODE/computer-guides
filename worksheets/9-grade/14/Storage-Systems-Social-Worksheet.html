Storage Systems: Social Worksheet

Grade 9 · Worksheet · About 25 minutes
Work with pen and paper. Use color pencils if you like. You do not need a computer or the internet.

Name: Grade/Section: Date:
How to use this worksheet
  • Do the parts in order from top to bottom.
  • Each part has a time so you know how long to spend.
  • Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: Storage choices affect privacy, cost, access, and the environment. Who controls the data and who can reach it are social questions too.

Do this:

  1. Name one risk of storing data in the cloud.
  2. Circle the careful choice: Encrypt private data · Upload everything unprotected

Part 2 – Privacy and control (about 5 minutes)

When data is in the cloud, a company holds it. Strong passwords and encryption protect it.

Real-life scenario: A student uploads personal photos without a password.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: What could happen?

Do this: Circle Safe or Risky.

ActionSafe or risky?
Strong password and two-step loginSafe · Risky
Sharing a passwordSafe · Risky
Encrypting private filesSafe · Risky
Public link to private filesSafe · Risky

Part 3 – Access for all (about 5 minutes)

Not everyone has fast internet. Cloud-only tools can leave some people out.

Real-life scenario: A class project needs the cloud, but a student has no internet at home.

Critical thinking: How could the class include that student?

Word bank: Access · Offline · Fair

Do this: Write the best word.

SituationWord
Everyone needs ...
An ... option helps
Choices should be ...

Part 4 – Digital footprint and waste (about 5 minutes)

Data centers use energy, and old drives become e-waste. We can delete what we do not need and recycle devices.

Real-life scenario: A student keeps 10,000 old photos on three drives.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: What is a responsible choice?

Do this:

  1. Write one way to reduce storage waste.
  2. Circle the responsible choice: Recycle old drives · Throw drives away

Part 5 – Responsible storage (about 5 minutes)

Do this: Draw a shield on a drive and a recycling symbol.

Write one rule for responsible storage.

Part 6 – Check what you learned (about 2 minutes)

Checklist. Put a ✓ in the box when it is true for you.

  • □ I protect private data.
  • □ I think about access.
  • □ I reduce waste.
  • □ I recycle devices.
  • □ I choose storage responsibly.

When all boxes are checked, you have reviewed the main ideas from this worksheet.
