Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: A storage system keeps data for later. Types include HDD, SSD, USB, optical discs, and cloud storage. They differ in speed, capacity, cost, and durability.

Do this:

  1. Name one device you use that stores data.
  2. Circle the storage with no moving parts: SSD · HDD

Part 2 – Types of storage (about 5 minutes)

An HDD uses spinning disks and is cheap per GB. An SSD uses flash memory and is faster and quieter. The cloud stores data on remote servers.

Real-life scenario: A laptop with an SSD starts faster than one with an HDD.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: Why might a company still buy HDDs?

Do this: Match each need with a type. Circle one.

Part 3 – Capacity and units (about 5 minutes)

Units grow by about 1000: KB, MB, GB, TB. Larger files need more capacity.

Real-life scenario: A 4 GB video fits on a 32 GB USB drive about eight times.

Critical thinking: How many 4 GB videos fit in 1 TB (1000 GB)?

Part 4 – Redundancy and backup (about 5 minutes)

RAID and backups protect against failure. The 3-2-1 rule: 3 copies, 2 media types, 1 offsite.

Real-life scenario: A school stores all grades on one drive. The drive fails.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: What would you change?

Do this:

  1. Write the 3-2-1 backup rule in your own words.
  2. Circle the safer plan: Backups in two places · One copy only
