## Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

**What you will learn:** **Data labeling** means adding tags to data so that people and programs understand it. A photo of a cat receives the label **cat**. Machine learning models learn from many labeled examples.

## Part 2 – Kinds of labels (about 5 minutes)

Labels may be **categories** (cat/dog), **numbers** (price) or **boxes** drawn around objects. The type depends on the task: classification, regression or detection.

**Real-life scenario:** A dataset has photos of fruit, each marked apple, banana or orange.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** What type of label is this?

## Part 3 – Quality of labels (about 5 minutes)

If labels are wrong, the model learns wrong. Clear **instructions**, **examples**, and a **second reviewer** improve quality.

**Real-life scenario:** A labeler marks a lemon as “orange” by mistake. The app later confuses them.

**Critical thinking:** How could the team reduce such errors?

## Part 4 – Label a small set (about 5 minutes)

A **training set** needs balance. If 95% of photos are cats, the model will favor cats. Add more variety.

**Real-life scenario:** A set has 90 cat photos and 10 dog photos.
