## Part 2 – Bias in labels (about 5 minutes)

If labelers always tag certain jobs with one gender, the model learns stereotypes. Diverse teams and clear rules reduce this risk.

**Real-life scenario:** A hiring app learned from labels that favored one group.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Who might be harmed?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Circle **Fair** or **Unfair**.

| Practice | Fair? |
|---|---|
| Review labels for bias | Fair · Unfair |
| Use one narrow group | Fair · Unfair |
| Include diverse people | Fair · Unfair |
| Ignore complaints | Fair · Unfair |

## Part 3 – People behind the labels (about 5 minutes)

Many labelers work long hours for low pay. Some see disturbing content. Ethical companies provide **fair pay** and **support**.

**Real-life scenario:** A worker labels violent images all day without breaks.

**Critical thinking:** What support does she need?

_________________________________________

## Part 4 – Privacy in datasets (about 5 minutes)

Datasets may include faces or voices. **Consent** and **anonymization** protect the people in them.

**Real-life scenario:** A company uses classroom photos to train a face model without asking.
