## Part 2 – Consent and privacy (about 5 minutes)

Participants should know what will be recorded, why, and how data is stored. They can stop at any time.

**Real-life scenario:** A researcher records screens. Before starting, she explains and asks permission.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** What could go wrong if a recording is shared without permission?

## Part 3 – Diverse participants (about 5 minutes)

Testing with a narrow group gives a narrow picture. Include different ages, abilities, languages, and devices.

**Real-life scenario:** A bank app is tested only by young adults. Older customers struggle.

**Critical thinking:** How could the team recruit a wider group?
