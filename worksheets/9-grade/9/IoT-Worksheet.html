Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: In this worksheet you will learn what the Internet of Things (IoT) is, how everyday devices can connect to the internet, and why connected devices can help people in homes, schools, and cities.

Part 2 – What is IoT? (about 5 minutes)

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects that have sensors, software, or communication tools so they can collect and share data. A smart thermostat, a fitness watch, or a connected security camera can all be part of the IoT.

Do this:

  1. Circle 2 examples of IoT devices:
    Smart thermostat     Notebook     Smartwatch     Pencil case
  2. Write one more example of an IoT device you have seen or heard about.

Part 3 – How IoT devices work (about 6 minutes)

An IoT device usually has four main parts:

  • Sensor: detects information such as temperature, movement, or light.
  • Processor: decides what the data means.
  • Connection: sends the data over a network such as Wi‑Fi or mobile data.
  • Action: responds by turning something on, sending a message, or storing data.

This creates a system in which devices can react to the world around them.

Part 4 – Benefits of IoT (about 5 minutes)

IoT can make life easier and more efficient. It can help people monitor energy use, track health, manage traffic, improve safety, and automate everyday tasks. In industry, IoT can make systems more efficient by collecting data in real time.

Do this:

  1. Choose the best answer: Which of these is a benefit of IoT?
    Growing food faster without sunlight     Saving energy by adjusting devices automatically     Making every device look the same
  2. In one short sentence, explain one way IoT might help a family or school.

Part 5 – Risks and challenges (about 4 minutes)

IoT also creates challenges. Connected devices can collect a lot of personal data. If the devices are not secure, they may be vulnerable to hacking or misuse. Also, some devices depend on internet access, so they may fail if the connection is weak or lost.

Do this:

  1. List 2 risks of connected devices.
  2. In one short sentence, why is it important to think about security when designing or using IoT devices?
