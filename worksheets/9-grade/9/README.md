# IoT – Worksheets (Grade 9, Week 9)

## For the teacher

- **Use:** Use these worksheets **whenever** the topic of the Internet of Things fits your curriculum. They are self-contained learning activities, not tied to a specific computer project.
- **Format:** Students work **offline**, with **pen and paper** (and color pencils if you like). No computer or internet needed.
- **Time:** About **25 minutes** per worksheet. Each part has a suggested time (roughly 3–6 minutes) so students can pace themselves.
- **Guidance:** The text is written so that students can complete it **on their own**; no teacher guidance is required during the activity.
- **Standalone value:** Each worksheet teaches on its own. No computer project is required; optional hands-on activities may complement them if you choose.

## Learning objectives

### Technical worksheet

- Explain the **Internet of Things** as a system of many sensing objects, not as a single smart gadget.
- Place a job on the device, a local controller, or a dashboard, including why a system should summarize data.
- Separate functions that must keep working locally from functions that can wait when a link fails.
- Judge whether a device message is specific enough for **interoperability**.
- Sketch a small building system and state a rule that survives a single broken link.

### Social worksheet

- Limit a public sensing system so a traffic benefit does not become routine tracking of people.
- Compare claims of the city, the vendor, and the people who are counted, including “anonymized” path data.
- Weigh energy use, replacement, and e-waste against how detailed the data is.
- Write a recommendation that names a benefit, a required limit, and an open question.
- Identify a group who should be consulted before a public IoT decision.
