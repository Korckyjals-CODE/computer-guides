# Efficiency – Worksheets (Grade 6, Week 13)

## For the teacher

- **Use:** Use these worksheets **whenever** the topic of efficiency fits your curriculum. They are self-contained learning activities, not tied to a specific computer project.
- **Format:** Students work **offline**, with **pen and paper** (and color pencils if you like). No computer or internet needed.
- **Time:** About **25 minutes** per worksheet. Each part has a suggested time (roughly 3–6 minutes) so students can pace themselves.
- **Guidance:** The text is written so that students can complete it **on their own**; no teacher guidance is required during the activity.
- **Standalone value:** Each worksheet teaches on its own. No computer project is required; optional hands-on activities may complement them if you choose.

## Learning objectives

### Technical worksheet

- **efficiency** means fewer steps with quality.
- Use **shortcuts**.
- A **template** helps.
- Plan a simple **workflow**.
- Compare two methods.

### Social worksheet

- Teams are efficient with **clear roles**.
- Share computers **fairly**.
- Rushing can cause **mistakes**.
- Use a **timer** or schedule.
- Write a fair agreement.
