## Part 2 – Teamwork and efficiency (about 5 minutes)

Teams are efficient when each member has a clear role and knows the goal.

**Real-life scenario:** Four students build a slideshow. One writes, one finds images, one designs, one checks.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** What happens if two students do the same job?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Circle **Helps** or **Slows**.

| Team action | Effect |
|---|---|
| Clear roles | Helps · Slows |
| No plan | Helps · Slows |
| Short check-ins | Helps · Slows |
| Everyone talks at once | Helps · Slows |

## Part 3 – Fair use of time (about 5 minutes)

Respecting time means arriving ready, finishing tasks on time, and not blocking others from using a computer.

**Real-life scenario:** One student keeps a computer for the whole class, and others wait.

## Part 4 – Balance (about 5 minutes)

Efficiency should not mean stress. A good balance includes rest, kindness, and quality.

**Real-life scenario:** A student rushes and makes many mistakes, then feels upset.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Why is speed not the only goal?

1. **Write** one way to be efficient and kind.
