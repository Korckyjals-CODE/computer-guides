## Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

**What you will learn:** **Efficiency** means doing a task using the fewest steps, little time, and good quality. With computers, efficient work uses shortcuts, templates, organized files, and a plan.

## Part 2 – Measuring efficiency (about 5 minutes)

We can compare methods by counting **steps** and **time**. Fewer steps with the same result is usually more efficient.

**Real-life scenario:** To rename five files, Ana does it one by one in 10 steps. Ben uses a pattern and finishes in 4 steps.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Which method is more efficient? What if Ben's pattern makes mistakes?

## Part 4 – Workflow planning (about 5 minutes)

A **workflow** is the order of steps in a task. A good workflow avoids repeated work and mistakes.
