Efficiency: Social Worksheet

Grade 6 · Worksheet · About 25 minutes
Work with pen and paper. Use color pencils if you like. You do not need a computer or the internet.

Name: Grade/Section: Date:
How to use this worksheet
  • Do the parts in order from top to bottom.
  • Each part has a time so you know how long to spend.
  • Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: Efficiency is also about people. Teams that communicate, divide tasks fairly, and respect each other's time use computers more efficiently and kindly.

Do this:

  1. Write one thing that slows a group down when working on a computer.
  2. Circle the better teamwork habit: Agree on roles · Everyone does everything

Part 2 – Teamwork and efficiency (about 5 minutes)

Teams are efficient when each member has a clear role and knows the goal.

Real-life scenario: Four students build a slideshow. One writes, one finds images, one designs, one checks.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: What happens if two students do the same job?

Do this: Circle Helps or Slows.

Part 3 – Fair use of time (about 5 minutes)

Respecting time means arriving ready, finishing tasks on time, and not blocking others from using a computer.

Real-life scenario: One student keeps a computer for the whole class, and others wait.

Critical thinking: How can a class share computers fairly?

Word bank: Timer · Schedule · Turns

Do this: Choose a fair tool.

SituationFair solution
Only 3 computers for 12 students
One student works slowly
Time is almost over

Part 4 – Balance (about 5 minutes)

Efficiency should not mean stress. A good balance includes rest, kindness, and quality.

Real-life scenario: A student rushes and makes many mistakes, then feels upset.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: Why is speed not the only goal?

Do this:

  1. Write one way to be efficient and kind.
  2. Circle the healthy goal: Good work at a steady pace · Fast work with errors

Part 5 – Team plan (about 5 minutes)

Do this: Draw a team of 3 with different roles at a computer.

Write one agreement for fair teamwork.

Part 6 – Check what you learned (about 2 minutes)

Checklist. Put a ✓ in the box when it is true for you.

  • □ I know teams are efficient with clear roles.
  • □ I can share computers fairly.
  • □ I know rushing can cause mistakes.
  • □ I can use a timer or schedule.
  • □ I can write a fair agreement.

When all boxes are checked, you have reviewed the main ideas from this worksheet.
