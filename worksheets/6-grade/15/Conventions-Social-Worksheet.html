Conventions: Social Worksheet

Grade 6 · Worksheet · About 25 minutes
Work with pen and paper. Use color pencils if you like. You do not need a computer or the internet.

Name: Grade/Section: Date:
How to use this worksheet
  • Do the parts in order from top to bottom.
  • Each part has a time so you know how long to spend.
  • Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: Conventions are also social. They show what a group agrees is polite or normal. Following conventions helps others feel comfortable.

Do this:

  1. Name a social convention, such as greeting a teacher.
  2. Circle why conventions exist: To help groups work smoothly · To make things confusing

Part 2 – Conventions in groups (about 5 minutes)

Groups have customs: greetings, turns in conversation, how to speak to elders. In computers, the same idea applies to naming and email.

Real-life scenario: A student writes a very casual message to a principal.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: How can conventions guide tone?

Do this: Circle Fits or Does not fit.

Part 3 – Cultural differences (about 5 minutes)

Conventions can change with culture. Dates can be written day-month-year in one place and month-day-year in another.

Real-life scenario: Two schools exchange files. The dates in the names do not match.

Critical thinking: What can they do?

Word bank: agree · explain · adapt

Do this: Fill in the best word.

SentenceWord
Both schools must ... on one style
We should ... our convention
We may need to ... for others

Part 4 – Changing a convention (about 5 minutes)

Conventions can evolve. A group may vote to improve them.

Real-life scenario: The class convention does not include the version number. Work gets confused.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: How might the class change it?

Do this:

  1. Write how the class can improve a convention.
  2. Circle the best way to change a convention: Discuss and vote · Ignore it

Part 5 – Community poster (about 5 minutes)

Do this: Draw a poster showing a convention your community values.

Explain why it matters.

Part 6 – Check what you learned (about 2 minutes)

Checklist. Put a ✓ in the box when it is true for you.

  • □ I know conventions guide groups.
  • □ I know conventions differ across cultures.
  • □ I can adapt my style.
  • □ I know conventions can change.
  • □ I can explain one convention.

When all boxes are checked, you have reviewed the main ideas from this worksheet.
