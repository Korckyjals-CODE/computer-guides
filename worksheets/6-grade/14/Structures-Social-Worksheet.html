Structures: Social Worksheet

Grade 6 · Worksheet · About 25 minutes
Work with pen and paper. Use color pencils if you like. You do not need a computer or the internet.

Name: Grade/Section: Date:
How to use this worksheet
  • Do the parts in order from top to bottom.
  • Each part has a time so you know how long to spend.
  • Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: The way we organize data affects people. Clear structures help teams work fairly, while messy ones cause mistakes and exclude those who cannot find information.

Do this:

  1. Name a time a messy list caused a problem.
  2. Circle the inclusive choice: Clear labels everyone can read · Secret codes

Part 2 – Clear structures (about 5 minutes)

Clear labels and consistent columns help everyone understand the data.

Real-life scenario: A table uses “C1”, “C2”, “C3” as headings. Nobody knows what they mean.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: How could the table be clearer?

Part 3 – Fair data (about 5 minutes)

Data should include everyone who needs to be counted.

Real-life scenario: A class list leaves out a new student.

Critical thinking: Who might be affected?

Word bank: Fair · Complete · Accurate

Do this: Write the best word.

SituationWord
The list should be ...
Every student must be ...
Correct names are ...

Part 4 – Teamwork on data (about 5 minutes)

Teams agree on one structure so everyone can add and read data.

Real-life scenario: Three students use different table layouts. Combining them takes hours.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: What should the team do first?

Do this:

  1. Write one rule for a shared table.
  2. Circle the better plan: Agree on columns first · Everyone does their own way

Part 5 – Our shared table (about 5 minutes)

Do this: Draw a shared table with columns the team agreed on.

Write why agreeing helps.

Part 6 – Check what you learned (about 2 minutes)

Checklist. Put a ✓ in the box when it is true for you.

  • □ I know clear structures help people.
  • □ I include everyone in data.
  • □ I agree on rules with my team.
  • □ I label things clearly.
  • □ I notice who might be left out.

When all boxes are checked, you have reviewed the main ideas from this worksheet.
