## How to use this worksheet

- Do the parts **in order** from top to bottom.
- Each part has a **time** so you know how long to spend.
- Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

## Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

**What you will learn:** The way we organize data affects people. Clear structures help teams work fairly, while messy ones cause mistakes and exclude those who cannot find information.

## Part 2 – Clear structures (about 5 minutes)

Clear labels and consistent columns help everyone understand the data.

**Real-life scenario:** A table uses “C1”, “C2”, “C3” as headings. Nobody knows what they mean.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** How could the table be clearer?

_________________________________________
