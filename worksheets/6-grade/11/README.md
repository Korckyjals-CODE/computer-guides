# Troubleshooting Steps – Worksheets (Grade 6, Week 11)

## For the teacher

- **Use:** Use these worksheets **whenever** troubleshooting steps fit your curriculum. They are self-contained; not tied to a specific computer project.
- **Format:** Offline, pen and paper (color pencils optional). No computer or internet needed.
- **Time:** About **25 minutes** per worksheet.
- **Guidance:** Students can complete the activities on their own.
- **Standalone value:** Each worksheet teaches on its own.

## Files

## Learning objectives

### Technical
- Explain what **troubleshooting** means and why order matters.
- Connect **symptoms** to basic first checks.
- Decide when to **restart**, **document**, or get help.
- Sketch a simple troubleshooting flow.

### Social
- Give calm, factual reports instead of **blame**.
- Help peers without taking over the whole fix.
- Propose fair team habits under time pressure.
- Design a class helper poster.
