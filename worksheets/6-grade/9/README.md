# Sensors – Worksheets (Grade 6, Week 9)

## For the teacher

- **Use:** Use these worksheets **whenever** the topic of sensors fits your curriculum. They are self-contained learning activities, not tied to a specific computer project.
- **Format:** Students work **offline**, with **pen and paper** (and color pencils if you like). No computer or internet needed.
- **Time:** About **25 minutes** per worksheet. Each part has a suggested time (roughly 3–6 minutes) so students can pace themselves.
- **Guidance:** The text is written so that students can complete it **on their own**; no teacher guidance is required during the activity.
- **Standalone value:** Each worksheet teaches on its own. No computer project is required; optional hands-on activities may complement them if you choose.

## Learning objectives

### Technical worksheet

- Explain a **sensor** as a device that measures a change and sends a **signal**.
- Match light, temperature, motion, and distance sensors to a concrete job.
- Describe a **threshold** and predict what happens when it is too sensitive or too slow.
- Separate **sensor**, **controller**, and **actuator** in a sense–decide–act loop.
- Identify causes of a **false reading** and a setup fix that does not require a new system.

### Social worksheet

- Explain how sensor settings can ignore people who are still, slow, or outside the sensor’s view.
- Distinguish a motion counter from a camera that stores video, and connect that difference to **consent**.
- Decide which uses of sensor data are acceptable and who should be allowed to see them.
- Choose where a school sensor belongs and where it does not.
- Write public questions a school should answer before installing sensors.
