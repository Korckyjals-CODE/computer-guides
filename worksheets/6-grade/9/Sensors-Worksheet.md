## Part 2 – What is a sensor? (about 5 minutes)

A **sensor** is a tool that detects something in the environment. It can measure **light**, **sound**, **temperature**, **movement**, or **distance**. A sensor then sends this information to a computer or controller so the system can respond.

## Part 3 – Types of sensors (about 6 minutes)

Different sensors are made for different jobs.

- A **light sensor** notices brightness or darkness.
- A **temperature sensor** notices hot or cold conditions.
- A **motion sensor** notices movement.
- A **sound sensor** notices noise or vibration.

These sensors help machines react in useful ways.

**Do this:**

1. **Match the sensor to the job.** Write the correct letter in each blank.

## Part 4 – Why sensors matter (about 5 minutes)

Sensors help systems make decisions. For example, a thermostat uses a temperature sensor to decide when to heat or cool a room. A robot uses motion and distance sensors to avoid bumping into objects. Without sensors, devices would not know what is happening around them.

**Do this:**

1. **Choose the best answer:** A thermostat wants to keep a room comfortable. Which sensor is most important?
   - Light sensor

   - Sound sensor

## Part 5 – Sensors and systems (about 4 minutes)

Sensors are part of a **system**. First, the sensor detects information. Next, the system processes that information. Then, the system takes an action. For example, a motion sensor notices movement, a controller decides what to do, and a light turns on.
