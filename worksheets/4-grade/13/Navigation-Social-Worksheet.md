## Part 2 – Staying safe while navigating (about 5 minutes)

Some links lead to unsafe pages. A safe navigator checks the address, avoids pop-ups, and asks an adult when unsure.

**Real-life scenario:** Sara gets a link that says “Free games.” She shows her teacher first.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Why should you ask before opening unknown links?
