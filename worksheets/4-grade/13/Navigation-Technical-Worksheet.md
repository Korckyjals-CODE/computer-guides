## Part 2 – Menus, links, and buttons (about 5 minutes)

A **menu** lists choices. A **link** takes you to another page. A **button** performs an action. Knowing the difference helps you navigate faster.

**Real-life scenario:** Eva wants her science notes. She clicks Documents, then the Science folder.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Why is it useful to know what each element does?

_________________________________________

| Description | Element |
|---|---|
| File, Edit, View at the top | Menu · Link · Button |
| Underlined text that opens a new page | Menu · Link · Button |
| A Save icon | Menu · Link · Button |

## Part 4 – Getting unlost (about 5 minutes)

If you get lost, use **Back**, **Home**, or search. Browser **tabs** let you keep several pages open, but too many tabs cause confusion.

**Real-life scenario:** Mia has 14 tabs open and cannot find her homework page.
