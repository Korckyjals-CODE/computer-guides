Navigation: Social Worksheet

Grade 4 · Worksheet · About 25 minutes
Work with pen and paper. Use color pencils if you like. You do not need a computer or the internet.

Name: Grade/Section: Date:
How to use this worksheet
  • Do the parts in order from top to bottom.
  • Each part has a time so you know how long to spend.
  • Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: We navigate not only computers, but also spaces online with other people. Respectful navigation means staying safe, respecting rules, and helping others find their way.

Do this:

  1. Write one rule you follow when you visit a website at school.
  2. Circle the safer choice: Ask before visiting a new site · Visit any link

Part 2 – Staying safe while navigating (about 5 minutes)

Some links lead to unsafe pages. A safe navigator checks the address, avoids pop-ups, and asks an adult when unsure.

Real-life scenario: Sara gets a link that says “Free games.” She shows her teacher first.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: Why should you ask before opening unknown links?

Do this: Circle Safe or Careful.

SituationAction
Class website from your teacherSafe · Careful
Unknown link in a chatSafe · Careful
Ad that says “Click now!”Safe · Careful

Part 3 – Helping others find their way (about 5 minutes)

People learn at different speeds. Good navigators explain steps clearly and patiently.

Real-life scenario: Ben shows a classmate how to find a shared folder. He says each step slowly.

Critical thinking: What makes an explanation easy to follow?

Word bank: Patience · Clear steps · Respect

Do this: Fill in what a good helper does.

SituationHelper action
Friend cannot find a file
Friend feels embarrassed
Friend asks again

Part 4 – Digital rules (about 5 minutes)

Schools and websites have rules about where we can go. Following them keeps everyone safe.

Real-life scenario: The school blocks a game website during class. Some students are upset.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: Why might a school block some sites?

Do this:

  1. Write one reason online rules are fair.
  2. Circle the responsible choice: Follow site rules · Hide your browsing

Part 5 – Our class navigation guide (about 5 minutes)

Do this: Draw a poster with two tips for safe and respectful navigating.

Write one tip you will follow.

Part 6 – Check what you learned (about 2 minutes)

Checklist. Put a ✓ in the box when it is true for you.

  • □ I know to ask before opening unknown links.
  • □ I can explain steps patiently.
  • □ I know why schools have online rules.
  • □ I can name two safe browsing habits.
  • □ I can help a classmate.

When all boxes are checked, you have reviewed the main ideas from this worksheet.
