## How to use this worksheet

- Do the parts **in order** from top to bottom.
- Each part has a **time** so you know how long to spend.
- Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

---

## Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

**What you will learn:** Naming rules work only when people **agree** and follow them. Rules help teams stay organized and show respect for everyone's time.

**Do this:**

1. **Name** one rule your class follows and why.

   _________________________

2. **Circle** the best way to make a rule: Agree together · One person decides

---

## Part 2 – Agreeing on rules (about 5 minutes)

A team should talk and agree. People are more likely to follow rules they helped create.

**Real-life scenario:** One student creates naming rules alone. Nobody follows them.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Why might the team ignore them?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Circle **Helpful** or **Not helpful**.

| Action | Helpful? |
|---|---|
| Discuss with the team | Helpful · Not helpful |
| Decide in secret | Helpful · Not helpful |
| Write the rule down | Helpful · Not helpful |
| Change it without telling | Helpful · Not helpful |

---

## Part 3 – Fairness (about 5 minutes)

Rules should be **fair**. They should work for everyone, including people who are new.

**Real-life scenario:** A rule says all names must be in a language one student cannot read.

**Critical thinking:** Is this fair? Why or why not?

_________________________________________

**Word bank:** *fair* · *clear* · *flexible*

**Do this:** Fill in the best word.

| Sentence | Word |
|---|---|
| Rules should be ... | _____ |
| They should be easy to ... | _____ |
| We can ... them if they do not work | _____ |

## Part 4 – Following and reminding (about 5 minutes)

If a friend forgets a rule, remind them **kindly**. Avoid blame.

**Real-life scenario:** A friend saves a file as “thing”.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** How do you remind them politely?
