## Part 2 – Fair turns and shared seats (about 5 minutes)

Stations and headphones are shared tools. Taking more than your turn, swapping seats without asking, or hogging a working mouse hurts classmates.

**Real-life scenario:** Only one mouse works well at a table. One student keeps it the whole period. Two partners cannot finish their research slides.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Write one fair sharing rule for that mouse.

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Mark **Fair** or **Unfair**.

| Action | Fair or Unfair? |
|--------|-----------------|
| Rotate the working mouse every 10 minutes | _____ |
| Hide headphones so only you can use them | _____ |
| Ask before switching to an empty station | _____ |
| Take over a classmate’s computer without asking | _____ |

## Part 3 – Help without taking over (about 5 minutes)

Helping is part of community. Show a partner where a menu is. Do not grab the mouse and finish their work for them. Ask, “Want a hint?” before you jump in.

**Real-life scenario:** Priya cannot find the save button. A classmate slides over, takes the mouse, saves the file, and closes the window before Priya can see how.

**Critical thinking:** How could the helper teach Priya instead of taking over?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Circle the better helper choice.

| Situation | Better choice |
|-----------|---------------|
| Partner is stuck on a menu | Point and explain · Grab the mouse and finish |
| You finish early | Offer quiet help · Play a blocked game |
| Someone breaks a rule by mistake | Remind kindly · Laugh loudly |

---

## Part 4 – When rules are broken (about 5 minutes)

Mistakes happen. Own them, fix what you can, and tell an adult when needed. Covering up a spill or a crack can put the next class at risk.

**Real-life scenario:** Omar bumps a bottle near a keyboard. A little water reaches the desk edge. He wipes with his sleeve and hopes no one notices.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** What should Omar do next to protect people and equipment?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Number these responsibility steps 1, 2, and 3.

| Step | Order (1, 2, or 3) |
|------|--------------------|
| Tell a teacher what happened | _____ |
| Stop using the wet area and keep others away | _____ |
| Notice the spill or damage | _____ |
