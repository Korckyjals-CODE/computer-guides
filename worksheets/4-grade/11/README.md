# Lab Rules – Worksheets (Grade 4, Week 11)

## For the teacher

- **Use:** Whenever the topic of computer lab rules fits your curriculum. Self-contained; not tied to a computer project.
- **Format:** Offline, pen and paper (color pencils optional). About **25 minutes** per worksheet.
- **Guidance:** Students can complete the activities on their own.
- **Standalone value:** Each worksheet teaches on its own.

## Learning objectives

### Technical
- Explain why **lab rules** protect people and equipment.
- Sort rules as people, equipment, or login/logout.
- Put **exit steps** in a sensible order.
- Handle lab equipment gently and report damage.
- Map enter/exit flow and label key rules.

### Social
- Treat lab rules as a **shared** responsibility.
- Practice fair sharing of seats, mice, and headphones.
- Help classmates without taking over.
- Report spills or damage honestly.
- Design a short lab community poster.
