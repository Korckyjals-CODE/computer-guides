**What you will learn:** Shortcuts save time, but they also affect **other people’s work**—especially paste, undo, and delete. Responsible use means asking before changing shared files and teaching shortcuts kindly.

## Part 2 – Shared docs and consent (about 5 minutes)

On a shared document, Ctrl+V can overwrite someone’s writing if you select their text first. Always **ask** or leave a comment before big changes.

**Real-life scenario:** Priya selects a whole teammate paragraph and pastes her own notes on top. The teammate’s work disappears from the view.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** What should Priya do instead?

_________________________________________

## Part 3 – Teaching shortcuts without showing off (about 5 minutes)

Knowing shortcuts is great. Boasting “You’re slow with the mouse!” is not. Offer to teach one shortcut at a time.

**Real-life scenario:** Chris finishes fast with shortcuts and laughs at classmates still using menus. One classmate stops asking for help.

**Critical thinking:** How can Chris share skill without making others feel small?

## Part 4 – Accidents and honesty (about 5 minutes)

If you Undo too far or Cut something by mistake, say so quickly. Honesty helps the team restore work.

**Real-life scenario:** Sam cuts a chart from a shared slide by accident and stays quiet. The team searches for ten minutes.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Why does speaking up early save time?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Circle **Yes** or **No**.

| After a shortcut mistake | Good choice? |
|---|---|
| Tell the group right away | Yes · No |
| Hope nobody notices | Yes · No |
| Try Undo / Version history with a teacher if needed | Yes · No |
