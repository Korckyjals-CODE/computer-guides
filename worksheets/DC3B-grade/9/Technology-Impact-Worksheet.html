Technology Impact: How New Tools Change Our World

DC3B · Worksheet · About 25 minutes
Work with pen and paper. Use color pencils if you like. You do not need a computer or the internet.

Name: Grade/Section: Date:
How to use this worksheet
  • Do the parts in order from top to bottom.
  • Each part has a time so you know how long to spend.
  • Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: In this worksheet you will learn how technology can change the way people live, work, and learn. You will also think about both the helpful effects and the challenges caused by new devices and tools.

Do this:

  1. Write 3 technologies that have changed daily life.
  2. In one short sentence, how has a new technology changed the way people do something?

Part 2 – What is technology impact? (about 5 minutes)

The impact of technology is the effect that technological tools have on people's lives, communities, and the environment. Technology can make work easier, connect people faster, or save time. It can also create problems, such as waste, screen time, or privacy concerns.

Do this:

  1. Circle 2 effects that technology can have:
    Makes life faster     Makes people more tired     Helps people communicate     Makes paper disappear
  2. Write one more example of a technology effect in daily life.

Part 3 – Helpful effects of technology (about 6 minutes)

Technology can help people in many ways. It can help us learn new information, organize tasks, communicate with family and friends, and solve problems. For example, phones make it easier to get information quickly, and online tools help people work together.

Do this:

  1. Match the technology to the help it gives. Write the correct letter in each blank.
    A. Phone   B. Calculator   C. Search engine
    - Quickly solves math problems:
    - Helps people find information:
    - Lets people call or message others:
  2. In one short sentence, which kind of technology do you think helps people the most and why?

Part 4 – Challenges of technology (about 5 minutes)

Technology does not only bring benefits. It can also create problems. Some systems use too much energy. Some devices distract people from learning or sleep. Some apps collect personal information, and not everyone knows how to use them safely.

Do this:

  1. Choose the best answer: Which is a challenge caused by technology?
    It can help people learn faster     It can distract people from schoolwork     It can help people stay in touch
  2. In one short sentence, explain one challenge that technology can cause in a home or school.

Part 5 – Thinking critically about technology (about 4 minutes)

When new technology appears, people should ask questions such as: Who benefits from it? Who might be harmed? Does it save time or waste time? Does it help people or create new problems? Thinking critically helps us use technology wisely.

Do this:

  1. Write 2 questions you could ask about a new technology before using it.
  2. In one short sentence, why is it important to think about both the benefits and the problems of a technology?

Part 6 – Check what you learned (about 2 minutes)

Checklist. Put a ✓ in the box when it is true for you.

  • □ I know that technology can have both positive and negative effects.
  • □ I can name at least one way technology helps people.
  • □ I can explain one challenge caused by technology.
  • □ I understand that we should think critically before using new technology.

When all boxes are checked, you have reviewed the main ideas from this worksheet.
