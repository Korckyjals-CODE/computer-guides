# Maintenance – Worksheets (Grade 5, Week 11)

## For the teacher

- **Use:** Whenever the topic of technology maintenance fits your curriculum. Self-contained; not tied to a computer project.
- **Format:** Offline, pen and paper (color pencils optional). About **25 minutes** per worksheet.
- **Guidance:** Students can complete the activities on their own.
- **Standalone value:** Each worksheet teaches on its own.

## Files

### Technical
- **Maintenance-Technical-Worksheet.html** / **.md**

### Social
- **Maintenance-Social-Worksheet.html** / **.md**

## Learning objectives

### Technical
- Explain what **maintenance** means for devices and systems.
- Sort physical vs digital maintenance tasks.
- Connect warning signs to care actions.
- Build a short maintenance schedule and map.

### Social
- Treat maintenance as a **shared** responsibility.
- Practice end-of-use habits and early reporting.
- Propose fair class care rules.
- Design a maintenance team poster.
