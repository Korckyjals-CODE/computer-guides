## Part 2 – Shared responsibility (about 5 minutes)

When many people use one laptop cart or lab, each person leaves the next person a working tool. That is **shared maintenance**.

**Real-life scenario:** After club time, three tablets are left with 5% battery and sticky screens. Morning class cannot start on time.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** What end-of-use habit would help the morning class?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Mark **Helps the group** or **Hurts the group**.

| Action after using a shared tablet | Helps or Hurts? |
|------------------------------------|-----------------|
| Return it to the cart and plug in the charger | _____ |
| Leave crumbs on the keyboard for the next student | _____ |
| Tell the teacher a case zipper is broken | _____ |

## Part 3 – Report early, without fear (about 5 minutes)

**Reporting early** is part of care. You do not have to “own” the blame to speak up.

**Real-life scenario:** Jordan notices a cracked charger tip on laptop #8. Jordan did not break it, but worries that speaking up will look like admitting fault.

**Critical thinking:** Write one respectful sentence Jordan could say to a teacher.

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Circle the better choice.

| What you notice | Better choice |
|-----------------|---------------|
| A fan is much louder than last week | Report it · Ignore it |
| Water near a keyboard (you did not spill it) | Tell an adult · Walk away quietly |
| A password dialog looks unusual | Ask a trusted adult · Type the password anyway |

---

## Part 4 – Fair class maintenance rules (about 5 minutes)

Fair rules say who cleans, who plugs in, and how long a turn lasts.

**Real-life scenario:** Two students argue over the only working mouse. One keeps it the whole period.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Write one fair rule for sharing and caring for lab tools.

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Number these end-of-class steps 1, 2, and 3.

| Step | Order (1, 2, or 3) |
|------|---------------------|
| Push in chairs and leave the space tidy | _____ |
| Save work and log out or close apps as instructed | _____ |
| Return devices to the cart and check chargers | _____ |
