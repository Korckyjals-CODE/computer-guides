Folders: Social Worksheet

Grade 5 · Worksheet · About 25 minutes
Work with pen and paper. Use color pencils if you like. You do not need a computer or the internet.

Name: Grade/Section: Date:
How to use this worksheet
  • Do the parts in order from top to bottom.
  • Each part has a time so you know how long to spend.
  • Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: Folders support teamwork and respect. Shared folders need clear rules on who can read, edit, or delete, so everyone's work is safe.

Do this:

  1. Name a folder your class shares.
  2. Circle the safest rule for shared folders: Ask before deleting · Delete what you do not need

Part 2 – Shared folders (about 5 minutes)

In a shared folder, many people can see or change files. Rules protect everyone's work.

Real-life scenario: A student deletes a team file by accident.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: What rule could prevent this?

Do this: Circle Good rule or Risky.

Part 3 – Permissions (about 5 minutes)

Permissions say who can view or edit a folder. Give only the access that is needed.

Real-life scenario: The teacher lets students view but not edit the answer key.

Critical thinking: Why limit access?

Word bank: View · Edit · Owner

Do this: Write the best word.

Part 4 – Fair teamwork (about 5 minutes)

Clear folders show who did what and help share credit.

Real-life scenario: A team folder names each file with the author's initials.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: How does this help fairness?

Do this:

  1. Write one way to show who made a file.
  2. Circle the fair choice: Put initials in names · Remove names

Part 5 – Team folder map (about 5 minutes)

Do this: Draw a shared folder with a place for each team member.

Write one rule your team would follow.

Part 6 – Check what you learned (about 2 minutes)

Checklist. Put a ✓ in the box when it is true for you.

  • □ I know shared folder rules.
  • □ I understand permissions.
  • □ I give credit fairly.
  • □ I protect others' work.
  • □ I help my team stay organized.

When all boxes are checked, you have reviewed the main ideas from this worksheet.
