## Part 2 – Roles beat chaos (about 5 minutes)

When everyone edits the same sentence at once, work collides. Assign who types, who researches, who checks.

**Real-life scenario:** Four students type in one shared cell. Sentences overwrite each other. Ten minutes vanish.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** How would roles make that time more efficient?

## Part 4 – Efficiency without exclusion (about 5 minutes)

The fastest typist should not shut others out. Teaching and rotating roles builds a stronger team than one “efficient” monopolizer.

**Real-life scenario:** Only Sam knows the shortcuts. Sam refuses to teach “because it’s faster if I do it.” Others learn nothing.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Why is teaching a teammate efficient for the long term?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Circle **Yes** or **No**.

| Choice | Inclusive efficiency? |
|---|---|
| Rotate who shares the screen | Yes · No |
| Hide tips so only you look skilled | Yes · No |
| Document steps for the group | Yes · No |
