## Part 2 – Speed vs accuracy (about 5 minutes)

Going faster is not efficient if you must redo the work. True efficiency balances **speed** and **accuracy**.

**Real-life scenario:** Two students write the same paragraph. One finishes first with five typos and spends ten minutes fixing. The other types a bit slower and is done once.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Who used time more efficiently? Why?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Circle **Often** or **Rarely**.

| Approach | More efficient? |
|---|---|
| Plan headings, then type | Often · Rarely |
| Type anything, fix chaos later always | Often · Rarely |
| Save early so you do not lose work | Often · Rarely |

## Part 4 – Reduce context switching (about 5 minutes)
