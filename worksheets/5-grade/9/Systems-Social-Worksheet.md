## Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

**What you will learn:** A system can work well for the people who planned it and still be hard for someone else. In this worksheet you will notice who a system fits, what happens when blame lands on a person instead of a missing part, and how a **fair rule** changes a shared system.

**Do this:**

1. **Circle** the system you want to think about: *Getting into school* · *A shared printer* · *Turning in work* · *The lunch line*

## Part 2 – Who the system was built for (about 5 minutes)

Designers often picture one kind of user. Anyone who moves at a different speed, arrives at a different time, or needs a different door can find that the system treats them as a problem. The system is the thing that can change.

**Real-life scenario:** Morning entry is a system: a bell, doors that lock on a timer, and a hallway direction. Most students arrive on the early bus and walk through before the lock. A student on a late bus, or a student who walks more slowly, reaches a locked door and has to find another way in.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Who was this system planned for? What part could change so the late or slower arrival is still a normal path, not an emergency?

## Part 3 – Fix the part, not only the person (about 5 minutes)

When a system fails, it is easy to say a person was careless. Sometimes that person did everything the system asked, and a part outside their control failed.

**Real-life scenario:** A homework system says “turn in by 4 p.m.” The student uploads the file at 3:40. The site shows an error until 4:10. The teacher’s list marks the work late. The student did not wait until the last minute.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** What failed, the student’s timing or a part of the system? What should the record say?

## Part 4 – A fair rule for a shared part (about 5 minutes)

A shared machine is a small system. Without a rule, one person’s long job blocks everyone else’s short job.

**Real-life scenario:** The class printer is one part that many students need. One student sends a 20-page picture with full color. Three others are waiting to print a one-page story that is due this period.

**Critical thinking:** Write one rule that lets the long job happen without stopping the short jobs that are due now.
