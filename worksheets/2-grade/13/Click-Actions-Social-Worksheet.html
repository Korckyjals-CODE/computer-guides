Click Actions: Social Worksheet

Grade 2 · Worksheet · About 25 minutes
Work with pen and paper. Use color pencils if you like. You do not need a computer or the internet.

Name: Grade/Section: Date:
How to use this worksheet
  • Do the parts in order from top to bottom.
  • Each part has a time so you know how long to spend.
  • Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: People use clicks every day to share, learn, and play. When we click, we make choices. Good choices keep us and others safe and respectful.

Do this:

  1. Write one thing you click on at school or home.
  2. Circle the safer habit: Think before you click · Click everything

Part 2 – Think before you click (about 5 minutes)

A click can open something helpful or something unsafe. We stop and think before clicking.

Real-life scenario: A pop-up says “You won a prize! Click here!” Mia does not click and tells her teacher.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: Why is it smart to ask an adult first?

Do this: Circle Click or Ask first.

SituationWhat to do

A pop-up that promises a prizeClick · Ask first
An unknown link in a messageClick · Ask first

Part 3 – Clicking with care (about 5 minutes)

Fast, careless clicking can delete work or open the wrong thing. Everyone benefits when we click carefully.

Real-life scenario: Eli clicks Delete without reading. His group's drawing disappears.

Critical thinking: How can we avoid deleting a friend's work by accident?

Word bank: Read · Ask · Undo

Do this: Fill in a good habit.

ProblemGood habit

Friend's work is on the screen

Part 4 – Sharing the mouse (about 5 minutes)

In groups, we take turns clicking and listen to each other's ideas.

Real-life scenario: Four friends work on one screen. Only one child clicks all the time.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: How can the group make it fair?

Do this:

  1. Write a turn-taking rule for your group.
  2. Circle the best rule: Everyone gets a turn · The fastest one clicks

Part 5 – Our class click rules (about 5 minutes)

Do this: Draw a poster with two click rules for your class.

Write the rule you will follow this week.

Part 6 – Check what you learned (about 2 minutes)

Checklist. Put a ✓ in the box when it is true for you.

  • □ I know to think before I click.
  • □ I ask an adult when I am not sure.
  • □ I click carefully so I do not delete work.
  • □ I take turns with the mouse.
  • □ I can name one click rule.

When all boxes are checked, you have reviewed the main ideas from this worksheet.
