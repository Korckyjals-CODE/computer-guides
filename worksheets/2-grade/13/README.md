# Click Actions – Worksheets (Grade 2, Week 13)

## For the teacher

- **Use:** Use these worksheets **whenever** the topic of click actions fits your curriculum. They are self-contained learning activities, not tied to a specific computer project.
- **Format:** Students work **offline**, with **pen and paper** (and color pencils if you like). No computer or internet needed.
- **Time:** About **25 minutes** per worksheet. Each part has a suggested time (roughly 3–6 minutes) so students can pace themselves.
- **Guidance:** The text is written so that students can complete it **on their own**; no teacher guidance is required during the activity.
- **Standalone value:** Each worksheet teaches on its own. No computer project is required; optional hands-on activities may complement them if you choose.

## Learning objectives

### Technical worksheet

- A **single click** selects.
- A **double click** opens.
- A **right click** shows a menu.
- Explain **drag and drop**.
- Click slowly and gently.

### Social worksheet

- To **think before I click**.
- Ask an adult when I am not sure.
- Click **carefully** so I do not delete work.
- Take **turns** with the mouse.
- Name one click rule.
