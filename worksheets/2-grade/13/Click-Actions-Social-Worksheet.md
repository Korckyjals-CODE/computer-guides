- Do the parts **in order** from top to bottom.

## Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

**What you will learn:** People use clicks every day to share, learn, and play. When we click, we make choices. Good choices keep us and others safe and respectful.

## Part 2 – Think before you click (about 5 minutes)

A click can open something helpful or something unsafe. We stop and think before clicking.

**Real-life scenario:** A pop-up says “You won a prize! Click here!” Mia does not click and tells her teacher.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Why is it smart to ask an adult first?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Circle **Click** or **Ask first**.

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Your teacher's learning page | Click · Ask first |
| A pop-up that promises a prize | Click · Ask first |
| An unknown link in a message | Click · Ask first |

## Part 3 – Clicking with care (about 5 minutes)

Fast, careless clicking can delete work or open the wrong thing. Everyone benefits when we click carefully.

**Real-life scenario:** Eli clicks Delete without reading. His group's drawing disappears.

**Critical thinking:** How can we avoid deleting a friend's work by accident?

_________________________________________

**Word bank:** *Read* · *Ask* · *Undo*

## Part 4 – Sharing the mouse (about 5 minutes)

In groups, we take turns clicking and listen to each other's ideas.

**Real-life scenario:** Four friends work on one screen. Only one child clicks all the time.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** How can the group make it fair?
