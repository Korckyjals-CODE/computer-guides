# Taking Care of Devices – Worksheets (Grade 2, Week 11)

## For the teacher

- **Use:** Use these worksheets **whenever** the topic of taking care of devices fits your curriculum. They are self-contained learning activities, not tied to a specific computer project.
- **Format:** Students work **offline**, with **pen and paper** (and color pencils if you like). No computer or internet needed.
- **Time:** About **25 minutes** per worksheet. Each part has a suggested time (roughly 3–6 minutes) so students can pace themselves.
- **Guidance:** The text is written so that students can complete it **on their own**; no teacher guidance is required during the activity.
- **Standalone value:** Each worksheet teaches on its own. No computer project is required.

## Learning objectives

### Technical worksheet

- Name a **device** and basic care items (case, charger, soft cloth).
- Describe **safe charging** and unplugging.
- Clean with a **soft, dry cloth** and store devices safely.
- Show charge, clean, and store steps.

### Social worksheet

- Recognize devices as **shared** resources.
- **Ask** before using someone else's device.
- Follow borrow-and-return steps with care.
- **Tell the truth** when something goes wrong.
- Create a short sharing-care sign.
