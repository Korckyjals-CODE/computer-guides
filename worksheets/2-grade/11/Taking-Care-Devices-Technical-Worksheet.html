Taking Care of Devices: Technical Worksheet

Grade 2 · Worksheet · About 25 minutes
Work with pen and paper. Use color pencils if you like. You do not need a computer or the internet.

Name: Grade/Section: Date:
How to use this worksheet
  • Do the parts in order from top to bottom.
  • Each part has a time so you know how long to spend.
  • Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: A device is a tool like a tablet, phone, or laptop. Taking care of devices means charging them safely, cleaning them gently, and storing them where they will not fall. In this worksheet you will name care steps for different devices.

Do this:

  1. Name one device you use at home or at school.
  2. Circle one care step you already know: Charging · Cleaning · Putting it away safely

Part 2 – Devices we use (about 5 minutes)

Different devices need care in similar ways. A tablet, a laptop, and a phone all have screens. Many need a charger. Some need a case or cover for protection.

Real-life scenario: Nora's tablet has a soft case. When it slips off a sofa cushion, the case softens the bump. Tomas's tablet has no case. The same bump leaves a crack on the corner.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: How can a case help a device?

Do this: Match each device with one care item from the word bank.

Part 3 – Charge safely (about 5 minutes)

A device needs power. Plug the charger into the wall and into the device. Do not yank the cord. Do not charge on a wet table. When the battery is full enough, you can unplug.

Real-life scenario: The class tablet beeps that the battery is low. Luis plugs it in, then pulls the cord hard when he needs the tablet again. The plug wiggles and feels loose.

Critical thinking: Why is pulling the cord hard a problem?

Do this: Write Safe or Not safe.

ActionSafe or Not safe?
Charge on a dry desk
Charge next to a spilled drink
Hold the plug (not the wire) when unplugging
Yank the cord from across the room

Part 4 – Clean and store (about 5 minutes)

Wipe a screen with a soft, dry cloth—not water sprayed straight onto the device, and not a rough towel. When you finish, put the device in its safe place (case, cart, shelf, or bag).

Real-life scenario: After snack, crumbs are on a keyboard. Eva brushes them off gently into a trash can. Marco sprays cleaning spray right onto the keys.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: Whose cleaning idea is safer for the device? Why?

Do this:

  1. Circle the better cleaner: Soft dry cloth · Wet paper towel dripping water
  2. Name one safe place to store a class tablet when work is done.

Part 5 – Draw your care steps (about 5 minutes)

Do this:

  1. Draw one device and label three care steps: charge, clean, store.
  1. Write one sentence about which step you will remember most.

Part 6 – Check what you learned (about 2 minutes)

Checklist. Put a ✓ in the box when it is true for you.

  • □ I can name a device and one way to care for it.
  • □ I know a case or cover can protect a device.
  • □ I can describe a safe way to charge and unplug.
  • □ I know to clean with a soft, dry cloth and store the device safely.
  • □ I can show charge, clean, and store in a drawing or list.

When all boxes are checked, you have reviewed the main ideas from this worksheet.
