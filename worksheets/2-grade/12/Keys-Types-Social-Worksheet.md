## Part 2 – Helping without taking over (about 5 minutes)

If a friend cannot find a key, you can **point** or **name** it. Grabbing their hands or typing for them without asking can feel unkind.

**Real-life scenario:** Kai cannot find the Enter key. Riley leans over, types the whole sentence, and says “Done.” Kai feels left out.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** How could Riley help without taking over Kai's turn?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Circle **Yes** or **No**.

| Help style | Kind? Circle |
|---|---|
| Point to Enter and say its name | Yes · No |
| Push the friend's hands away and type alone | Yes · No |
| Ask, “Want a hint or should I show you?” | Yes · No |

## Part 3 – Fair turns with special keys (about 5 minutes)

Special keys like **Space**, **Enter**, and **Backspace** are used by everyone. Hogging the keyboard so others cannot fix a mistake is not fair.

**Real-life scenario:** Two partners write a class list. One child keeps holding Backspace and deleting the other's words for fun.

**Critical thinking:** Why is deleting a partner's words without asking a problem?

_________________________________________

**Do this:**

1. **Write one fair rule** for using Backspace with a partner.

   _________________________

2. **Circle** the fair habit: Ask before changing their text · Erase their work as a joke

---

## Part 4 – Patience while others hunt for keys (about 5 minutes)

Finding letter and number keys takes practice. **Waiting quietly** and offering a calm tip helps classmates feel safe to try.

**Real-life scenario:** Nora searches for the number 8. Max sighs loudly and says “Hurry up!” Nora feels nervous and presses the wrong key.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** What patient words could Max use instead?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Circle **Patient** or **Not**.

| Action | Patient or Not? |
|---|---|
| Wait and smile while a friend looks | Patient · Not |
| Tap the desk and say “Too slow!” | Patient · Not |
| Offer, “Numbers are in the top row.” | Patient · Not |
