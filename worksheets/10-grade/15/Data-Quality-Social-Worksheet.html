Data Quality: Social Worksheet

Grade 10 · Worksheet · About 25 minutes
Work with pen and paper. Use color pencils if you like. You do not need a computer or the internet.

Name: Grade/Section: Date:
How to use this worksheet
  • Do the parts in order from top to bottom.
  • Each part has a time so you know how long to spend.
  • Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: Data quality is also a people issue. Errors in records can deny services, damage reputations or waste resources. Everyone who touches data shares responsibility.

Do this:

  1. Name one record where an error could hurt someone.
  2. Circle who owns quality: Everyone who handles the data · Only IT

Part 2 – Impact of errors (about 5 minutes)

A wrong address can delay medicine. A misspelled name can block a certificate. Poor data hurts real people.

Real-life scenario: A student's grade is typed as 4.0 instead of 40.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: What harm could result?

Do this: Circle High impact or Low impact.

Part 3 – Shared responsibility (about 5 minutes)

Data stewards, teachers and students all have roles: enter carefully, report mistakes, and verify before sharing.

Real-life scenario: A teacher notices a wrong grade but thinks someone else will fix it.

Critical thinking: What should she do?

Word bank: report · verify · correct

Do this: Fill in the best word.

SentenceWord
If I see an error I ...
Before sharing I ...
The owner will ... the data

Part 4 – Honesty and correction (about 5 minutes)

Admitting a data mistake builds trust. Hiding it can cause bigger problems.

Real-life scenario: A student discovers an error in a survey he published.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: What is the ethical response?

Do this:

  1. Write how you would announce a correction.
  2. Circle the better response: Publish a clear correction · Hope nobody notices

Part 5 – Trust poster (about 5 minutes)

Do this: Draw a poster about everyone's role in data quality.

Write one promise.

Part 6 – Check what you learned (about 2 minutes)

Checklist. Put a ✓ in the box when it is true for you.

  • □ I know errors can hurt people.
  • □ I share responsibility.
  • □ I report mistakes.
  • □ I verify before sharing.
  • □ I support honest correction.

When all boxes are checked, you have reviewed the main ideas from this worksheet.
