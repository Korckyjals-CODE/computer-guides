## Part 2 – Dimensions of quality (about 5 minutes)

**Accuracy**: values are correct. **Completeness**: nothing missing. **Consistency**: same format everywhere. **Timeliness**: up to date. **Validity**: follows rules. **Uniqueness**: no duplicates.

**Real-life scenario:** A table lists age = -5, a blank email and the same student twice.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Which dimensions fail?
