## Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

**What you will learn:** Data quality is also a **people** issue. Errors in records can deny services, damage reputations or waste resources. Everyone who touches data shares responsibility.

## Part 2 – Impact of errors (about 5 minutes)

A wrong address can delay medicine. A misspelled name can block a certificate. Poor data hurts real people.

**Real-life scenario:** A student's grade is typed as 4.0 instead of 40.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** What harm could result?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Circle **High impact** or **Low impact**.

| Error | Impact |
|---|---|
| Wrong medical allergy | High impact · Low impact |
| Extra space in a name | High impact · Low impact |
| Wrong exam result | High impact · Low impact |
| Slightly different font | High impact · Low impact |

## Part 3 – Shared responsibility (about 5 minutes)

Data stewards, teachers and students all have roles: enter carefully, report mistakes, and verify before sharing.

**Real-life scenario:** A teacher notices a wrong grade but thinks someone else will fix it.

**Critical thinking:** What should she do?

Admitting a data mistake builds **trust**. Hiding it can cause bigger problems.
