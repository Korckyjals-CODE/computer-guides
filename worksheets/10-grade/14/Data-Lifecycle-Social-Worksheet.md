## Part 2 – Consent (about 5 minutes)

People should know what data is collected and agree to it.

**Real-life scenario:** An app collects location data without asking.

**Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing:** Why is this a problem?

_________________________________________

**Do this:** Circle **Respectful** or **Not respectful**.

| Practice | Respectful? |
|---|---|
| Ask clearly before collecting | Respectful · Not respectful |
| Hide data collection | Respectful · Not respectful |
| Collect only what is needed | Respectful · Not respectful |
| Sell data secretly | Respectful · Not respectful |
