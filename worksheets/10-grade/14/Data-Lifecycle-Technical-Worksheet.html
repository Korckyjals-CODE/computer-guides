Data Lifecycle: Technical Worksheet

Grade 10 · Worksheet · About 25 minutes
Work with pen and paper. Use color pencils if you like. You do not need a computer or the internet.

Name: Grade/Section: Date:
How to use this worksheet
  • Do the parts in order from top to bottom.
  • Each part has a time so you know how long to spend.
  • Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: The data lifecycle describes the stages data goes through: create, store, use, share, archive, and delete. Managing each stage keeps data useful, safe, and legal.

Do this:

  1. Name one piece of data you created this week.
  2. Circle the final stage of the lifecycle: Delete · Create

Part 2 – The stages (about 5 minutes)

Data is created, stored, used, shared, archived when rarely needed, and finally deleted.

Real-life scenario: A school creates grades, stores them in a database, shares reports, archives old years, and deletes records after the legal period.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: Why is the final stage important?

Do this: Match each action with its stage. Circle one.

ActionStage
Entering a new recordCreate · Store · Archive · Delete
Saving in a databaseCreate · Store · Archive · Delete
Moving old files to long-term storageCreate · Store · Archive · Delete

Part 3 – Quality and retention (about 5 minutes)

Data should stay accurate and be kept only as long as needed. A retention policy sets how long.

Real-life scenario: A company keeps customer data for 10 years though the policy says 5.

Critical thinking: What risks does this create?

Word bank: Accurate · Retention · Archive

Do this: Write the term that matches.

DescriptionTerm
Correct and up to date
Rule for how long to keep
Long-term storage

Part 4 – Secure deletion (about 5 minutes)

Deleting a file may not erase it. Secure deletion overwrites data so it cannot be recovered.

Real-life scenario: A school sells old computers without wiping them.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: What should be done first?

Do this:

  1. Write one reason to securely delete data.
  2. Circle the safer step: Wipe the drive · Just empty the recycle bin

Part 5 – Lifecycle diagram (about 5 minutes)

Do this: Draw a circle diagram with the six stages and arrows.

Write one risk at one stage.
