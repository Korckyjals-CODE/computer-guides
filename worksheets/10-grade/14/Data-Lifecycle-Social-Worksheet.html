Data Lifecycle: Social Worksheet

Grade 10 · Worksheet · About 25 minutes
Work with pen and paper. Use color pencils if you like. You do not need a computer or the internet.

Name: Grade/Section: Date:
How to use this worksheet
  • Do the parts in order from top to bottom.
  • Each part has a time so you know how long to spend.
  • Read every instruction before you write. You can do this on your own.

Part 1 – What you will learn (about 3 minutes)

What you will learn: Data about people has a life too. Respecting privacy, consent, and the right to delete data builds trust between organizations and people.

Do this:

  1. Name one app that holds data about you.
  2. Circle the respectful practice: Ask for consent · Collect everything

Part 2 – Consent (about 5 minutes)

People should know what data is collected and agree to it.

Real-life scenario: An app collects location data without asking.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: Why is this a problem?

Do this: Circle Respectful or Not respectful.

PracticeRespectful?

Part 3 – Right to delete (about 5 minutes)

Many laws let people ask companies to delete their data.

Real-life scenario: A user asks an app to delete their account and data.

Critical thinking: How should the company respond?

Word bank: Consent · Delete · Privacy

Do this: Write the best word.

SituationWord
People give ...
People can ask to ...
Respect for ... matters

Part 4 – Trust and transparency (about 5 minutes)

Organizations should explain how long they keep data and why.

Real-life scenario: A school posts a clear privacy notice for families.

Discuss with a partner or reflect in writing: How does this build trust?

Do this:

  1. Write one thing a privacy notice should say.
  2. Circle the better practice: Clear notice · Hidden rules

Part 5 – Data and people (about 5 minutes)

Do this: Draw a person with a lock and a trash can showing they control their data.

Write one right people have over their data.

Part 6 – Check what you learned (about 2 minutes)

Checklist. Put a ✓ in the box when it is true for you.

  • □ I know consent.
  • □ I know the right to delete.
  • □ I value transparency.
  • □ I protect privacy.
  • □ I respect people's data.

When all boxes are checked, you have reviewed the main ideas from this worksheet.
